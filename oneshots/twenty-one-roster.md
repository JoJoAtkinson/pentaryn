# Twenty-One — NPC & Monster Roster (design pass)

Ten-scene one-shot, D&D 5e (2024 rules). Villain: **Ozmandius the Unmade ("Oz")** — himself in Scenes 1, 9, and 10; in every scene between he wears one host and gets **nothing for it but the host's own stat block**. This is a **planning document for hand-building Foundry actors** — plain portable stat blocks, all rolls pre-computed.

**Calibrated against:** 4 level-5 PCs (baseline), scaling down to 2. Party reference: AC 12–21, ~32–65 HP, attacks +6/+7 (+9 Thessa), save DCs 15. Chassis grounded in SRD 5.2 / MM 2024: Bandit (AC 12, 11 HP), Bandit Captain (AC 15, 52 HP), Guard (AC 16, 11 HP), Tough (AC 12, 32 HP), Berserker (trimmed, hooded), Commoner (AC 10, 4 HP).

**Scale lines:** every monster carries `scale: 2=N, 3=N, 4=N` — exact headcount at party size 2/3/4. `4=` is the baseline design. Oz hosts and unique NPCs are always `2=1, 3=1, 4=1` (a `0` means "omit at that party size").

**Scenes 1 and 6 have no combat at all.** That's deliberate, and their entries below say so — don't pad them.

---

## OZ'S VESSEL — the reusable possession template

Apply to the scene's host. The rule is absolute and it is the whole design: **Oz gets the host's stat block and nothing else.** No AC bonus, no bonus HP, no extra attacks, no save advantage, no unlocking reactions scene over scene. A possessed innkeeper hits like an innkeeper. The menace is *who is looking out of the eyes*, not what the body can do — his real stat block waits in Scene 10.

- **On 0 HP — Oz abandons the host.** Black frost spiderwebs out of the mouth and eyes; a voice not the host's mutters *"Twenty… not twenty-one."* The host collapses **unconscious but stable at 0 HP** — alive unless the party deliberately executed them (moral texture: every host is a victim, not a villain).
- **Scenes 2–3 — dropping the host does NOT end the scene.** Oz hops to another body *in the same scene*: a bandit's eyes go wrong mid-sentence, a villager in the crowd goes still. This is deliberate — it teaches the rule at the table.
- **Scene 4 onward — dropping the host ends the scene.** No hop. Say it with the frost and let the scene close.
- **Telltales (every host):** a **signet ring** — a circle cut into the band that **stops just short of closing** — on the host's hand. Auto-found on a search, Perception DC 14 if the hand is visible. It is **never on the body afterward**. In SUBTLE/HIDDEN scenes his social front is **Deception +7**; Insight DC 15 catches the wrongness (dead-eyed micro-pauses, archaic word choices).

## Scene 1 — Run Twenty (the trophy room)

**Oz himself. NO COMBAT — do not roll initiative, do not stat anything.** The cold open: Oz among the trophies of twenty runs, then the reboot. Whatever the party tries — talk, steel, spells — the scene ends in the reboot; don't reach for the Scene 10 block, it fires first. Nothing to build for Foundry here.

---

## Scene 2 — The Road to Town (Cliff Trade Road)

**Oz = Rennick the Knife (OBVIOUS by the end — the hop is the lesson).** A roadside stickup, deliberately trivial: this is the **tutorial fight**. Let it be easy; the teaching beat is what happens when Rennick drops.

### Rennick the Knife (Oz's Vessel)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: 2024 Bandit Captain. **AC 15** · **HP 52** · Speed 30 · **CR 2** — the template adds nothing; that's the point.
- **Multiattack:** two attacks — **Scimitar** +5, 1d6+3 slashing, or **Light Crossbow** +5, 1d8+3 piercing (80/320).
- **Parry** (reaction): +2 AC against one melee attack that would hit.
- **Bark an Order** (bonus action): one bandit within 30 ft uses its reaction to make one attack.
- **When he drops, Oz hops** (Scene 2–3 rule): frost out of Rennick's mouth — and a surviving bandit's eyes go wrong mid-sentence. Make the party watch it happen.
- Tactics: enjoying the stickup too much; quotes the party's own words back at them from runs they don't remember.

### Road Bandit — Cutter · Hobbes · Grin · Little Ned · Squint · Toad
scale: 2=4, 3=5, 4=6 — trim names from the front of the list at smaller tables, but **Little Ned is never omitted: Scene 3 needs his corpse.**
- **AC 12** · **HP 11** · Speed 30 · **CR 1/8** — (2024 Bandit)
- **Scimitar** +3, 1d6+1 slashing, or **Light Crossbow** +3, 1d8+1 piercing (80/320)
- Tactics: nobodies. They break and run when Rennick frosts over and two of theirs are down. **Little Ned dies in this fight one way or another** — if the party pulls every punch, he catches a stray bolt in the rout. Scene 3 is his funeral.

## Scene 3 — The Market Square (Fairfield Market)

**Oz = Big Ned (OBVIOUS).** A lynching, not a battle. Little Ned came home over a saddle, his older brother has the square on his side, and there's rope in the crowd. **The threat is the Mob Temperature ladder, not statblocks.**

### Big Ned (Oz's Vessel)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: himself. **AC 13** · **HP 45** · Speed 30 · **CR 3** — the template adds nothing.
- **Felling Axe** +5, 1d12+3 slashing. One attack — he's a woodcutter, not a soldier.
- Ned is not the fight; the square is. He swings only at whoever gets between him and the rope, and every swing feeds the mob.
- **When he drops, Oz hops** (Scene 2–3 rule, second and last time): a villager in the crowd goes still — and the temperature **jumps one step**: the square just watched armed strangers cut down a grieving man. Dropping the host doesn't end this scene; talking does.

### Mob Temperature (the actual encounter — a ladder, not statblocks)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1  *(one track, any party size)*
- **1 — Muttering.** Room to talk.
- **2 — Closing in.** The square becomes difficult terrain; the exits clog.
- **3 — Hands on.** Each PC in the press at the start of their turn: mob clump +4, DC 12 Str save or **grappled**.
- **4 — The rope comes out.** The mob rolls initiative. Run it as clumps: **every ~4 villagers act as one mob attack** — +4 to hit, 2d4 bludgeoning, and DC 12 Str save or grappled.
- **Cooling it:** each named villager the party talks to **honestly** steps the ladder back one. Intimidation DC 15 scatters one clump but doesn't lower the temperature — fear isn't calm.
- **Killing townsfolk is a story loss, not an encounter feature.** Any lethal AoE into the square jumps the temperature straight to 4.

## Scene 4 — The Short Drop (Town of Hanged Men)

**Oz = the magistrate (OBVIOUS).** Reads the party's own sentence back to them.

### Magistrate Corben Vale (Oz's Vessel)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: Commoner. **AC 10** · **HP 4** · Speed 30 — the template adds nothing; Vale is exactly as killable as any magistrate.
- **Gavel** +2, 1d4 bludgeoning — he will never use it.
- Social front: Deception +7; Insight DC 15. The signet ring is on the hand holding the writ.
- **Dropping him ends the scene** — this is the first scene where the rule is live. One crossbow bolt through the magistrate ends the hanging: the town watches him exhale black frost, and nobody wants to be deputized anymore. The guards and the crowd are the fight only if the party won't take that shot.
- Tactics: stays on the scaffold, uses height and the guards as a screen. Wants the hanging to proceed; hides behind procedure when it can't.

### Scaffold Guard
scale: 2=2, 3=3, 4=4
- **AC 16** (chain + shield) · **HP 11** · Speed 30 — (SRD Guard)
- **Spear** +3, 1d6+1 piercing (melee or 20/60)
- Tactics: hold the scaffold stairs; they're townsfolk in uniform — morale breaks if the executioner drops.

### Executioner
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- **AC 13** · **HP 52** · Speed 30 — (Berserker chassis, hooded)
- **Greataxe** +5, 1d12+3 slashing
- **Hood's Swing** (Recharge 5–6): sweeping arc — every creature within 5 ft, DC 13 Dex save, 1d12+3 slashing (half on success).
- Tactics: guards the lever. Doesn't pursue off the scaffold.

### Deputized Crowd (escalating mob — rules-light, not fixed monsters)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1  *(one escalation track, not a headcount)*
- If the alarm bell rings, **2 deputized townsfolk arrive at the end of each round** (max 12). AC 10, HP 4, club +2, 1d4.
- Run them as clumps: **every 4 townsfolk act as one mob attack** — +4 to hit, 2d4 bludgeoning, and DC 12 Str save or grappled.
- **Off-ramps (say yes to these):** Intimidation DC 15 scatters one clump; dropping Vale ends the whole scene (Scene 4+ rule) — escalation included.
- Killing townsfolk is a story loss, not an encounter feature.

## Scene 5 — Last Orders (Dragonsfall Tavern, three floors)

**Oz = the innkeeper (SUBTLE).** Exits barred before the party noticed.

### Harl Wetherby, Innkeeper (Oz's Vessel)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: himself. **AC 15** · **HP 47** · Speed 30 · **CR 5** — his own sheet; the template adds nothing.
- **Cleaver** +7, 2d8+4 slashing. **One attack per round, always** — he is testing them, not fighting. The CR is real; the restraint is the point.
- Tactics: keeps polishing a glass while the mercenaries break in; only swings when someone touches the bar or the barred door. Talks the whole time — knows their names.
- **Dropping him ends the scene** (Scene 4+ rule); the mercenaries are the actual fight.

### Doorbreaker Mercenary
scale: 2=3, 3=4, 4=6
- **AC 14** (chain shirt) · **HP 32** · Speed 30 · **CR 1** — (Tough chassis, armored up)
- **Battleaxe** +5, 1d8+3 slashing, or **Heavy Crossbow** +3, 1d10+1 piercing (100/400)
- **Break the Line** (1/fight): after hitting with the axe, shove as a bonus action — DC 13 Str save or pushed 10 ft / knocked prone.
- Tactics: come through the door two abreast behind a bench-ram, split to flank the biggest weapon. Real muscle — they focus-fire whoever drops one of theirs.

## Scene 6 — The Quiet Night (Hamlet of Grimsby)

**NO COMBAT — deliberately.** Oz is one of **16 named villagers** and **does nothing**: no attacks, no plots, no moves. Any of them could be him; the only tells are the template's — the signet ring, Deception +7 against Insight DC 15. The scene's tension is the search, not a fight, so there is nothing to build here beyond 16 Commoner tokens. If the party starts hurting villagers to flush him out, that's the story loss, not an encounter. Dropping the right villager ends the scene (Scene 4+ rule); dropping a wrong one is just a murder Oz gets to watch.

## Scene 7 — The Fire (Spider's Tear Opera House, three levels)

**Oz = the lead tenor (SUBTLE — until the doors won't open).** He locked every door in the house before the curtain; the scripted Act III fire does the killing. **The threat is the crowd crush — run it as a hazard, not statblocks.** Escape is **up**: boxes → catwalks → out through the skylight.

### The Lead Tenor (Oz's Vessel)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: Commoner. **AC 10** · **HP 4** · Speed 30. Performance +8; Deception +7 (template); Insight DC 15. The signet ring flashes when he gestures on the high notes.
- **He never attacks.** He sings Act III straight through the smoke, note-perfect, until the catwalks burn.
- **Dropping him ends the scene** (Scene 4+ rule) — if the party can reach the stage through a thousand panicking bodies.

### The Crush (hazard — the scene's real killer)
- **Fire schedule (scripted):** at the Act III cue the stage ignites; at the end of each round the fire spreads one zone outward. A creature starting its turn in a burning zone takes **2d6 fire**; the main floor is smoke-choked (heavily obscured) from round 4.
- **Crowd clusters** (≈5 patrons each): **AC 10** · **HP 20 per cluster** · Speed 30 — a cluster hitting 0 is dispersal, fear, **not five corpses**.
- **Crush zones** (aisles, the locked doors): a PC starting their turn in a packed zone — clump attack +4, 2d4 bludgeoning, and DC 12 Str save or knocked **prone** and carried 10 ft toward the doors that will not open.
- **Redirecting:** Intimidation or Persuasion DC 15 (action) turns one cluster toward the box stairs — the actual way out. Every cluster redirected is lives saved; count them out loud.
- **Lethal AoE into the crowd is a story loss** — the party becomes the fire's accomplices.
- **The way out is up:** main floor → boxes (DC 12 Athletics, climb the rail) → catwalks (DC 12 Acrobatics, burning) → skylight. A failed leg costs 2d6 fire or a fall back down one level. Carrying a civilian adds +2 to each DC — that's the heroic play; make it visible.

## Scene 8 — The Guide (Catacombs of Silence, 30×40)

**Oz = "Tam Bellows," the hired lantern-bearer (SUBTLE).** He never fights. He steers — every "shortcut" crosses something already living down here.

### "Tam Bellows," Lantern-Bearer (Oz's Vessel — never fights)
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- Host base: Commoner. **AC 10** · **HP 4** · Speed 30. Deception +7 (template); Insight DC 15. The signet ring is on his lantern hand, lit every time he raises the light.
- **He never attacks.** His weapon is the route: across the broken seal's rim ("mind the step"), under the Remnants' roost with the lantern held high, always one turn ahead of whatever wakes.
- **Dropping him ends the scene** (Scene 4+ rule) — but anything already woken stays woken; the Warden doesn't care whose scene it is.

### Warden of the Unfinished Circle — ⭐ what's living down here
scale: 2=1, 3=2, 4=2
- Large **construct** (ward-flesh: verdigris bronze poured over old sealing-script; it rises out of the broken circle when a creature crosses onto it)
- **AC 17** · **HP 68** · Speed 30 · Immune: poison; charmed, frightened, exhaustion, paralyzed · Resist: cold, necrotic
- **Circle-Bound:** cannot move farther than 30 ft from the seal's rim; **regains 5 HP** at the start of its turn while touching the circle (destroyed normally at 0).
- **Multiattack:** two **Sealing Strikes** +7, 2d8+4 force; once per turn on a hit, DC 15 Str save or dragged **10 ft toward the circle's center**.
- **Branding Grasp** (bonus action, one creature it hit this turn): DC 15 Con save or 2d8 necrotic and the creature takes a **Half-Mark** — a burning half-circle brand. *A Half-Marked creature has **disadvantage on its first save against Oz's possession Lunge in Scene 10.*** (Ends on Remove Curse / Greater Restoration, or when Oz falls.) — This is the load-bearing hook: tonight's wounds are the finale's weakness.
- **Closing Arc** (Recharge 5–6): 15-ft cone of black frost, DC 15 Dex save, 4d8 cold (half on success); the ground in the cone ices over (difficult terrain until end of the Warden's next turn).
- Tactics: drag one PC onto the circle and brand them; they treat only creatures *on or over the circle* as trespassers — stepping fully off the rim buys a round of peace (teach the party this, it's how a 2-PC table survives). The constructs **ignore Tam** — he reads as part of the seal, which should bother the party before the fight, not after.

### Seal Remnant (script-wisps)
scale: 2=2, 3=2, 4=4
- Small **construct** · **AC 13** · **HP 17** · Fly 30 (hover) · Immune/resist as Warden
- **Frost Lash** +6, 2d6 cold
- **Unravel** (on death): bursts into cold script — creatures within 5 ft, DC 13 Con save or 1d6 cold.
- Tactics: harry the back line so the Wardens can drag the front line.

## Scene 9 — The Parley (the tower Library)

**Oz himself — alone, no host, no tricks.** No intended combat; this scene is the conversation. But if the party attacks, he fights at **full strength**: run the complete **Ozmandius the Unmade** block from Scene 10 — 250 HP, AC 17, CR 10, Twenty Deaths reactions, all three legendary actions, everything.

scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1
- **He does not die here.** At 0 HP nothing resolves — the program will not resolve its antagonist outside the final encounter. The wounds simply stop mattering: he gets up, straightens himself, and **walks upstairs**. Let the party land the killing blow and watch it not count — that's what the scene is for.
- Save the every-round Lunge cadence for Scene 10 — here he doesn't need anyone else's body, and he knows it.

## Scene 10 — Twenty-One (the Trophy Gallery — his real body, and this time it can end)

**Ozmandius's true form — and killable at last**, because the Trophy Gallery holds **no NPCs for him to wear**: nothing to hop to, no host to abandon, only the party and the trophies of twenty runs. Solo, legendary-tier, genuinely lethal; target ~3–4 rounds against a focused party of 4.

### Ozmandius the Unmade
scale: 2=1, 3=1, 4=1  *(solo boss — tune with the table below, never with headcount)*
- Medium **aberration** · **AC 17** · **HP 250** · Speed 40 · **CR 10**
- Saves: Str +4, Dex +6, Con +7, Int +6, Wis +7, Cha +9, and **advantage on all saving throws**
- Immune: cold, necrotic; charmed, frightened, exhaustion, possession · Darkvision 120 ft
- On his chest: the **unfinished circle**, glowing at the gap — twenty tally-scars orbit it. The ring's cut circle, written in flesh.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack:** three **Unmaking Touches** +8, reach 5 ft, 2d8+5 necrotic each. (Ranged option: **Frost Shard** +8, range 60 ft, 2d10+5 cold.)

**The Lunge (possession attempt — fires every round, automatic)**
- At **initiative count 20** (losing ties), Oz's shadow tears loose and lunges at one creature he can see within 40 ft: **DC 15 Charisma save** (DC 16 once he's Bloodied). A Scene-8 **Half-Mark** imposes disadvantage on that creature's *first* Lunge save. The party's bodies are the only ones left in the room — this is his way out, and he tries it every round.
- **On a failure — Oz-Ridden** until the end of the creature's next turn:
  - On its turn, **Oz moves the PC and makes one weapon attack with it** (the PC's own to-hit/damage) against a target of Oz's choice. Oz **cannot** spend the PC's limited resources — no spell slots, no Smites, no superiority dice, no Rage (base attacks only).
  - Oz's true body stays active, but he **can't use Twenty Deaths reactions** while riding (he's split — this is the window).
- **Cures — how the table dogpiles a rescue instead of a kill (any one ends it immediately):**
  - An ally within 5 ft uses an **action** to grab, shake, or call the PC back → the PC **repeats the save with advantage**.
  - The Oz-Ridden PC **takes any damage** → immediate re-save (no advantage). *(One clean slap works; do not murder your friend.)*
  - *Protection from Evil and Good* on the PC ends and prevents it; *Remove Curse* / *Dispel Evil and Good* ends it.
  - Otherwise it **ends automatically** at the end of the PC's next turn — *"Twenty-one bodies. None of them fit."*
- If a PC drops to 0 HP while Oz-Ridden, Oz feeds: he **regains 20 HP**. (Deliberately punishes "just kill the possessed guy.")

**Twenty Deaths — full strength.** Oz has **two reactions per round**, each named reaction usable once per round:
- ***"I remember this"*** (at-will): an attack that hits him is rerolled; use the lower.
- ***"You always do that"*** (3/fight, each distinct feature only once): negate one class feature's effect as it lands.
- ***"Not this time"*** (3/fight): a failed save becomes a success. *(This is his legendary-resistance analog — he hoards them for Hypnotic Pattern-class saves.)*

**Legendary actions — 3/round, one at a time at the end of another creature's turn:**
- **Frost Step (1):** teleport up to 30 ft; black frost blooms at both ends.
- **Unmaking Touch (2):** one attack.
- **Grasp of Twenty (1):** skeletal frost hands erupt under one creature within 30 ft — DC 15 Str save or **restrained** until the end of its next turn.

**Bloodied (125 HP) — "The Circle Opens":** the gap in his chest-brand tears wider. Lunge DC becomes **16**, and Frost Step becomes free (doesn't count against his 3 legendary actions) once per round. He stops monologuing.

**Tactics:** round 1 Lunge targets a Half-Marked PC; Touches go into whoever is easiest to reach after Frost Step; *You always do that* is saved for Smites and Sneak Attack; he fights like someone who has already seen every version of this fight except one.

**Solo-boss tuning table (use this, not headcount):**

| Party size | HP | Multiattack | Legendary actions | "Not this time" | Lunge cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 PCs | 250 | 3 attacks | 3/round | 3/fight | every round |
| 3 PCs | 200 | 2 attacks | 2/round | 2/fight | every round |
| 2 PCs | 150 | 2 attacks | 2/round | 2/fight | every **other** round |

*Math check (4 PCs): focused party output vs AC 17 with "I remember this" shaving runs ~65–80/round → 250 HP ≈ 3.5 rounds. Oz's output (3 Touches + legendary Touch ≈ 56/round potential, plus a stolen PC attack on Lunge rounds) will down a PC who fights sloppy — lethal the whole time, as intended.*
