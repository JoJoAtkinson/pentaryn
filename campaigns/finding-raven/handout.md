# Finding Raven

> She was right about the Drow. So she went where the answers are.

## The short version

- **Caven died in the Brenfen Fen and came back wrong**, his soul not quite in his body. He and Raven met in the ruins under Stonewatch, two necromancers drawn to the same door, and she has worked on his problem beside him ever since.
- **They disagreed only about the Drow.** Caven went down alone looking for answers and was taken. Raven was right.
- **Now Raven is missing**, and the others came into the Underdark to get Caven out because no one knows her better. He believes she has gone after the leads they worked on together:
  - **The Brenfen Fen:** where he died. If something went wrong when he came back, it happened there.
  - **The Amethyst Dragon's Cave:** a dragon that died of old age, its soul still clinging to the stone. It might show how a soul stays bound to a body. Raven gave him a shard from there.
  - **The ruins under Stonewatch**, a town at the foot of Lone Peak: where they met, and the sealed door they never got past. Someone in town may have seen her.

## In Caven's words

I died out on the fen, and I came back. Ever since, I have driven this body the way I drive my dead: from somewhere else, a long way off. Raven has been working on that with me since the day we met. Every book, every experiment, every late night. She never once made it feel like a burden.

We only disagreed about the Drow. I was sure their libraries could tell me whether a soul stays tied to a body that has died. She said they wouldn't, and that going would cost more than it was worth. I went anyway, without her. A drow house took me, and I learned the hard way that she was right.

Now she is missing. That is why they came down into the dark for me: no one knows her like I do. I know she hasn't given up on me. She has done what I should have done and followed the real leads, the ones we worked on together. There are three.

### 1. The Brenfen Fen

The peat flats where I grew up, a fen warden's son, with a rundown cottage out among the reeds. The fen takes bodies slowly and gives them back wrong. It is where I died, and where I pulled myself back from between the planes.

Raven and I went over that night again and again: where it happened, what the ground was like, what I saw. My father's book still has my name in it, the night I went in. If something went wrong when I came back, it happened there, and if any trace of it is left, it is in that ground. I never wanted to go back.

### 2. The Amethyst Dragon's Cave

A cave far from any road, where an old amethyst dragon went to die. It died of old age, nothing more. By the time Raven and I found it, it was long dead, but its violet light still glowed in the stone like embers.

We worked there together. A dragon does not die all at once, and this one had not fully left. Some of it still clung to the body and the stone. We studied how a soul stays bound to a body, and what holds it there. Raven broke a shard from the glowing stone and pressed it into my hand. We left before we had our answer, and I meant to go back. She would have gone back to finish it.

The shard still glows. Some nights I would swear it is brighter.

### 3. The Ruins under Stonewatch

Stonewatch sits at the foot of Lone Peak, and under the town are older ruins. Deep inside is a sealed door carved in an ancient script. I went down to find it, and so did she. That is where Raven and I met: two necromancers who had followed the same rumour to the same door.

If we read the glyphs right, what lies behind it are souls bound to the world, held here for as long as those ruins have stood. We came to call them the Anchored. We took a rubbing and worked over it together for weeks, and we never got past that door. It is my question exactly: what keeps a soul here, and can it be tied back? Someone in Stonewatch will have seen her if she went down.

---

Three places. She went ahead, the way she always does. I mean to catch up.
