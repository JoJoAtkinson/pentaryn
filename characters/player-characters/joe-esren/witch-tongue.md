# The Hollow Cant — Esren's Witch-Tongue

*Vholzha* — "the Hollow's speech." The language Esren mutters when he casts. It is not
a real language; it is a **deterministic cipher over English** plus a short lexicon of
irregular *words of power*, so any new spell can be converted in under a minute and the
result will always come out the same way twice.

Everything is written **phonetically** — say it exactly as spelled, using the
pronunciation key below.

---

## The 30-second recipe (new spell → incantation)

1. **Say what the spell does in 2–4 English words, command first.**
   Not the spell's name — its *intent*. "Swallow the light." "Rot the flesh." "Heed my word."
2. **Drop the little words** — the, a, an, of, to, my, your, their, in, at, and.
3. **Check the Words of Power lexicon first.** If a word is there, use that form.
4. **Run everything else through the cipher** (tables below): digraphs first, then single letters.
5. **Optional dressing:** open with **Vholeth** *(by the Hollow)*, close with a whispered
   **nakht** *(it is night)*.

> **Example:** Darkness → "swallow the light, drown the day" → *swallow light, drown day* →
> **Svollau lakht, draun dei** → full form: *"Vholeth… svollau lakht, draun dei — nakht."*

---

## The cipher

### Step 0 — prep

- Lowercase the word.
- Drop a final silent **e** (*curse* → curs, *face* → fac). Keep the e if it's the word's
  only vowel (*be* stays be).

### Step 1 — digraphs (longest match first, left to right; converted chunks are final — never re-convert output)

| English | becomes | says | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| igh | **akh** | *ahkh* (guttural) | night → **nakht**, light → **lakht** |
| tion | **zhan** | *zhahn* | — |
| ch | **kh** | *ch* in "loch" | charm → **khorm** |
| wh | **v** | v | whisper → **vuspar** |
| ph | **v** | v | — |
| gh | **kh** | guttural | — |
| qu | **kv** | kv | — |
| ck | **k** | k | trick → **truk** |
| wr | **vr** | vr | rewrite → **ravrut** |
| sh, th, ng | *unchanged* | — | already witchy — keep and hiss them |
| ee, ea | **ei** | *ay* (day) | dream → **dreim**, sleep → **sleip** |
| oo | **u** | *oo* (moon) | blood → **blud** |
| ou, ow, aw | **au** | *ow* (how) | wound → **vaund**, shadow → **shodau** |
| ai, ay | **ei** | *ay* | — |
| ie | **ai** | *eye* | friend → **vraind** |

### Step 2 — single letters

**Vowels rotate darker:**

| English | becomes | says |
|---|---|---|
| a | **o** | *oh* |
| e | **a** | *ah* |
| i | **u** | *oo* |
| o | **au** | *ow* |
| u | **y** | *i* in "myth" |
| y (as vowel) | **i** | *ee* |

**Consonants — three shifts, rest unchanged:**

| English | becomes | note |
|---|---|---|
| w | **v** | the signature sound of the Cant |
| f | **v** | soft breath turns to venom |
| j | **zh** | *s* in "measure" |
| c | **k** — but **z** when the original spelling had c before e/i/y | cat-c goes hard, face-c goes hissing: face → **voz**, trace → **troz** |
| b d g h k l m n p r s t v x z | unchanged | |

### Pronunciation key (for reading the output)

- **a** = ah · **e** = eh · **i** = ee · **o** = oh · **u** = oo · **y** = *i* in "myth"
- **au** = *ow* in "how" · **ei** = *ay* in "day" · **ai** = *eye*
- **kh** = *ch* in "loch"/"Bach" (back of throat) · **zh** = *s* in "measure"
- **Stress the first syllable. Always.** Let the end of every word fall away to breath.

## Words of power (irregular lexicon)

The cipher is for everyday words. Words the Cant considers *sacred or dangerous* have
older, irregular forms — learned by rote, never derived. This is also the escape hatch:
**when the cipher spits out something silly or too English-sounding, coin a word of power
instead and record it here.** (The canonical example: *dark* → "dork." No.)

| English | Hollow Cant | says | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Hollow (the void between) | **Vhol** | VOHL | source of his shadow magic |
| by the Hollow *(opener)* | **Vholeth** | VOHL-eth | optional first word of any casting |
| it is night *(seal)* | **nakht** | NAHKHT | optional last word, always whispered; regular form of *night*, promoted to ritual use |
| dark / the darkness | **nakhtor** | NAHKH-tor | coined from *nakht* — because the cipher yields "dork" |
| Sorrel | **Ssorau** | SSOR-ow | his lost wife's name in the Cant. He does not say it aloud. If he ever casts with it, something is very wrong — or very right. |

*Add rows as the campaign coins them. Keep this table short — if everything is irregular,
nothing is.*

## Grammar of an incantation

- **Command first, object after:** *Svollau lakht* — "swallow (the) light."
- **No little words.** Articles, pronouns, prepositions vanish; the listener's dread fills
  the gaps.
- **Address the world, not the target.** The Cant gives orders to reality: "rot the
  flesh," never "I cast Inflict Wounds."
- **Length signals weight.** Cantrip = 3 words muttered. Level 1 = 3 words with intent.
  Level 2 = 4 words + the seal. Something desperate = opener + phrase + seal, each word
  slower than the last.
- **The second breath.** To lengthen any incantation, either hang a modifier after the
  noun (*vaurd vor* — "the word, far") or add a second command after a dash
  (*Raut vlash, vuthar baun* — "rot the flesh, wither the bone"). Second commands land
  harder.
- **Clipped casting.** At-will magic thrown in a hurry may be clipped to its first two
  words (*Spluntar mund!*). Leveled spells are never clipped — the Hollow hears a
  half-spoken bargain as an insult.
- **Subtle Spell** = no incantation at all. The words are still there — spoken on the
  inside of his teeth.

### Delivery (how to sound like dark magic)

- **Whisper-forward.** Witches don't shout. Volume low, consonants over-articulated.
- **Hiss every s and sh.** Hold them a half-beat too long: *ssvollau*.
- **kh from the back of the throat**, like clearing away something that crawled in.
- **Roll the r's** if you can.
- **First syllable hits, the rest falls.** Every phrase ends on a downward, dying pitch —
  the seal word *nakht* should be barely audible, mostly breath.

## Esren's spellbook, converted

Worked from the recipe — these are canon; say them the same way every time.

### Cantrips (muttered — may be clipped to the first two words when cast in a hurry)

|---|---|---|---|
| Message | whisper the word far | **Vuspar vaurd vor** | VOO-spahr VOWRD VOR |
| Mind Sliver | splinter the mind — pain | **Spluntar mund — pein** | SPLOON-tahr MOOND — PAYN |
| Minor Illusion (sound) | false sound — hear it | **Vols saund — heir** | VOHLS SOWND — HAYR |
| Minor Illusion (image) | false form — see it | **Vols vaurm — sei** | VOHLS VOWRM — SAY |

### Level 2 (worth the full dressing: *Vholeth… ⟨phrase⟩ — nakht.*)

| Spell | Intent | Incantation | says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darkness | swallow the light, drown the day | **Svollau lakht, draun dei — nakht** | SVOHL-low LAHKHT, DROWN DAY — NAHKHT |
| Pass without Trace | step in shadow — no trace | **Stap shodau — nau troz** | STAHP SHOH-dow — NOW TROHZ |
| Suggestion | heed my word — obey | **Heid vaurd — aubai** | HAYD VOWRD — OW-buy |
| Tasha's Mind Whip | lash the mind — break | **Losh mund — breik** | LOHSH MOOND — BRAYK |

### Background boon

| Spell | Intent | Incantation | says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modify Memory | forget… rewrite the memory | **Vaurgat… ravrut mamauri — nakht** | VOWR-gaht… RAHV-root mah-MOW-ree — NAHKHT |

Note the shared roots doing quiet work: *mund* (mind) recurs across Mind Sliver and Mind
Whip; *vaurd* (word) across Message and Suggestion; *nakht/lakht* rhyme night against
light. That recurrence is what makes it read as a language at the table instead of noise.
