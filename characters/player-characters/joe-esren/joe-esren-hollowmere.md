## Specials

### Resourceful · *Human*

After every Long Rest I gain **Heroic Inspiration**: I can expend it to **reroll a d20** immediately after rolling and must use the new roll. I can only hold one at a time.

### Modify Memory · *Vaurgat… ravrut mamauri — nakht* · *background boon — once per day*

**Modify Memory** — normally a 5th-level spell I can't yet cast — **once per day**, as a feature of my background.

[full text ↗](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:modify-memory)

**Action · 30 ft · WIS save (DC 13) · Conc 1 min**
*Rewrite a creature's memory of a recent event.*
One creature I can see fails a WIS save (it has Advantage if we're fighting) → **Charmed + Incapacitated**, aware of nothing but my voice. While it holds, I can reshape its memory of one event from the **last 24 hours** that lasted **≤10 min** — erase it, sharpen it to perfect clarity, change the details, or plant a new one. Ends with **no effect** if the target takes damage or is hit by another spell, and it must understand my language. *Remove Curse* or *Greater Restoration* restores the true memory.

### Lucky [   ] [   ]

 **Luck Points.** You have a number of Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a Long Rest.

**Advantage.** When you roll a d20 for a D20 Test, you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself Advantage on the roll.

**Disadvantage.** When a creature rolls a d20 for an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose Disadvantage on that roll.

### Crafter · *Origin feat*

[full text ↗](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:crafter)

**Tool Proficiency.** Proficient with three Artisan's Tools — I took **Tinker's**, **Weaver's**, and **Woodcarver's Tools**.
**Discount.** 20% off any nonmagical item I buy.
**Fast Crafting.** After a Long Rest I can craft **one** item from the list below (need the matching tools + proficiency). It lasts until my next Long Rest, then falls apart.

Fast-craft options for my tools:

- **Tinker's** → Bell, Shovel, Tinder Box
- **Weaver's** → Basket, Rope, Net, Tent
- **Woodcarver's** → Club, Greatclub, Quarterstaff

### Shadow

http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/sorcerer:shadow-sorcery

**Eyes of the Dark.** Darkvision 120 ft, Blindsight 10 ft. I can see through my own darkness.

**Strength of the Grave.** If you would drop to 0 Hit Points and not die outright, you can make a Charisma saving throw (DC 5 + the damage taken). On a successful save, your Hit Points instead change to a number equal to your Charisma modifier + Sorcerer level. After you succeed on this save, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest. *(No effect vs radiant damage or a critical hit.)*
