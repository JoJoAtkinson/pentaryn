## Spells

**Subtle Spell** *(1 pt)* — cast it without any Verbal, Somatic, or Material components

**Twinned Spell** *(1 pt)* — raise the spell's effective level by 1

**Innate Sorcery** *(Bonus Action · 1 min · 2 per Long Rest)* — while active: **+1** to my spell save DC **and Advantage on my Sorcerer spell attack rolls**.

**Font of Magic** *(Sorcery Points: 5)* — Spell slot → points (no action) = points equal to the slot's level. Points → spell slot (Bonus Action): **2 pts = L1**, **3 pts = L2**, **5 pts = L3** (created slot vanishes on a Long Rest). The trade is lossy on purpose — a L2 slot converts to only 2 points but costs 3 to rebuy, so never cycle. Cap: **never more than 5 points**, so spend down before a Short Rest or Sorcerous Restoration gives nothing.

### Sorcerous Restoration · *level 5 class feature* [   ]

When I finish a **Short Rest**, I regain expended **Sorcery Points** up to half my Sorcerer
level, rounded down — **2 points** at level 5. Once used, I can't use it again until a Long
Rest. Two points is exactly one Subtle Spell plus one Twinned Spell, or one 1st-level slot
out of Font of Magic, bought back every short rest.

http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/sorcerer:shadow-sorcery

**Eyes of the Dark.** Darkvision 120 ft, Blindsight 10 ft. I can see through my own darkness.

**Strength of the Grave.** If you would drop to 0 Hit Points and not die outright, you can make a Charisma saving throw (DC 5 + the damage taken). On a successful save, your Hit Points instead change to a number equal to your Charisma modifier + Sorcerer level. After you succeed on this save, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest. *(No effect vs radiant damage or a critical hit.)*
