# Raven's Trail (GM only)

Three places only Caven knows, where Raven, before her lich rite, would really go. Each one is
dangerous. Raven floats: the GM places her at whichever of the three suits how the session
progresses, and she is dropped in as a lich near the end (the reveal for the two-shot). The order
is open too; a hint at one place can lead to the next.

**Why Caven is in the Underdark party at all** (decided 2026-11-05): Raven went missing, and the
others came into the Underdark to get Caven out of the drow cells, because no one knows her
better. He had gone down alone, over Raven's objection, to search the drow libraries. In Caven's
view she was right about the Drow, and her disappearance means she followed the real leads.

**Player handout:** [[campaigns/finding-raven/handout|Finding Raven]], Caven's side, player-safe.

**The through-line:** the party is seeing what she left behind. Each spot proves Caven really does
know Raven well: he knows where she would go and why. And every place reads to Caven as Raven
trying to cure him.

**Nothing proves she was there.** Each place gives a reason to go and a challenge, never proof of
Raven either way.

## The Amethyst Dragon's Cave

- **How Caven knows:** he was there with Raven (decided 2026-11-05). The dragon had died of old age;
  they studied how its soul lingered in the body and the stone, and Raven gave him the Shattered
  Amethyst Dragon Shard from it. **What he doesn't know:** after he was taken, Raven brought the
  dragon back. He expects a dead dragon; he finds the half-alive thing below.
- **The dragon:** CR 12, built from parts (none of the owned books has an amethyst dragon). Raven
  turned it against its will into something in between: half alive, half not alive, half a soul.
  Her experiment in splitting soul from body, and it mirrors Caven's own problem.
- **Its mood: upset and angry.** It has emotions, and they are pain and rage.
- **The shard sets it off.** Caven carries a piece of it, and that makes it lash out at him and the
  party: "You carry my piece." Its violet glow matches the shard and the light in Raven's lich
  ribcage.
- **Option:** the missing half of its soul is in the shard. Give it back and the dragon dies whole,
  or keep it as a living example of a split soul.
- **Stat block ideas:** base it on Adult Brass, Adult White or Shadow Dragon (CR 13, MM), brought
  down to CR 12. Split breath (half force, half necrotic), flickering between alive and undead,
  a psionic voice.

## Where Caven Died

- **Why Raven would go:** to her, Caven is a lich who never finished: someone who came back without
  a phylactery or a plan. Where he died is the closest thing she has to a finished experiment.
- **The danger:** many failed experiments, her constructs. The main threat is a group of stone
  golems (Stone Golem, CR 10, MM) reskinned as her failed vessels, each readable as her method:
  plain faceless stone, then bog flesh pressed into stone, then one with Caven's proportions.
- **Their mood: none.** They are just things, no emotions. They stand still, undisturbed, until
  something triggers them.
- **What they are for:** empty vessels searching for a soul. They do not know how to merge with
  one.
- **The cottage:** a rundown cottage in the swamp holds what is left of her research. Reading it, Caven learns what
  she was trying to do.
- **The trigger is an accident.** Raven did not set a trap; it just worked out that way. The
  golems wake at the same moment Caven realises what they are for and how they work.
- **Space:** a swamp with a rundown cottage, outside under the sky, to contrast with the cave and
  the ruins.
- **Budget:** Stone Golem is 5,900 XP. Three is 17,700, over the High budget of 14,100. Two Stone
  Golems plus a Flesh Golem (1,800) is 13,600, right at High. Or three, each weakened.
- **The swamp: the Brenfen fen** (decided 2026-11-05). The fen gives bodies back wrong; his father
  was a fen warden there.

## Deep in the Ruins: the Anchored

- **Where:** under [[world/factions/groundstruck/_overview|Ulearos]] (Lone Peak), the fallen flying
  city. The goliaths live in and on the mountain, never under it. The swallowed ruins are down
  there, out of anyone's sight since about 2552.
- **What is down there:** the Anchored, Ao Sathanum souls bound to the world. They are no longer
  really people.
- **What Caven knew:** this is where he and Raven **met** (decided 2026-11-05): two necromancers
  who followed the same rumour to the same sealed door, carved in the Ancient script. They took a
  rubbing and worked over it together, but never got past the door. He did not know how the souls
  are bound; his interest was *that* they were bound to the world, and why. He understood them to
  be passive. "The Anchored" is their own name for them.
- **Why he goes now:** it is where they met, and the door they never got past.
- **What happened:** the binding was stolen. It does not prove Raven was there or not.
- **The creature:** effectively a wraith whose bindings were stolen. Unlike the other two, it is
  mad and insane and just attacks. Stat block: a Death Knight Aspirant (CR 11, MM, 7,200 XP),
  reskinned as an Ancient.
- **It slew an entire town: Stonewatch** (decided 2026-11-05), the town at the mountain's foot. The ruins run under Stonewatch itself; Caven knows them as "the ruins under Stonewatch" and does not know the town is dead.
- **Two parts:**
  1. **The aftermath.** The party arrives at a destroyed town: bodies everywhere.
  2. **The creature.** They find the monster in the middle of the destruction.
- **Talo's stake** (from his Foundry biography): he is Groundstruck, born on Ulearos, walking home.
  His brother died learning to fly before finding his star: a soul that may be lost between the sky
  and the world, unable to go home. The Groundstruck punishment for that is being locked away from
  the sky, underground, and Talo gave it to himself. The Anchored are souls held under the mountain,
  cut off from the sky for 3,200 years: his people's taboo, and his brother's fate made real.
- **Open for Talo:** with the binding gone, are the Anchored freed to go home to the sky, or lost
  between? Same question as his brother's soul, and as Caven's. Don't settle it at the table.

## Design at a glance

| | Dragon's Cave | Where Caven Died | Deep in the Ruins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | A split soul (her test) | A body without a soul (her vessels) | A soul held past its time (the anchor) |
| **Mood** | Upset, angry; the shard sets it off | None; still until an accident triggers them | Mad; just attacks |
| **Mirror for Caven** | What he is | What he might need | What happens when the binding goes |
| **Shape of the fight** | One big creature, aimed at Caven | Several tough bodies, slow grind | Aftermath first (a slain town), then the creature |
| **Pressure on the party** | Breath and its flicker between alive and undead | Magic Resistance and Slow against a party of three casters | Three attacks a turn, Hellfire Orb, Parry |
| **Way out besides killing** | Give back the shard's half of its soul | Don't trigger them | None: it is mad |
| **Space** | Natural cave, violet light | A swamp with a rundown cottage, outside under the sky | Ancient architecture under Ulearos, deep and dark |
| **Whose story** | Caven (the shard) | Caven (his death) | Caven and Talo (his mountain, his brother) |

The party is three full casters: Marrith (Sorcerer, fire), Caven (Wizard, Abjurer), Talo (Druid,
Stars, Stone Goliath). Stone Golems and the Aspirant both have Magic Resistance.
