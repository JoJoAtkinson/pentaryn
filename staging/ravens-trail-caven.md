# Where Raven Would Go: Caven's three guesses

Player-safe. Written from what Caven knows: nothing here about the lich rite, the golems, the
stolen binding, or the dead town. GM notes for each place are in [[ravens-trail]].

Raven was looking for the same thing Caven is: where his soul went when he came back, and how
to put it back in his body. If she has gone somewhere, she has gone to finish that work. Caven
knows only three places where that work could be finished.

---

## 1. The Amethyst Dragon's Cave

*Maps: Dranthek's Twilight (the way in), Ruins of Umbral Light Temple (the lair).*

A cave far from any road, where an old amethyst dragon went to grow weak. Its light still
lingers in the stone, a violet glow that pulses slowly, like breathing.

**Why Caven thinks she went there:** the shard he carries came from this cave. Raven put it in
his hand herself. She told him the dragon was fading and that the magic still in it was too
rare to waste. She meant to use it for an experiment, and she would not say what for. Raven
never wasted words. If she described the place to him, she meant to go back. A dragon's soul
is very large and very old. If anything could show her how a soul sits in a body, or how it
comes loose, it is this one.

**What he carries:** the shard still glows. It has never gone out, and lately it seems
brighter, as if it knows where it came from.

---

## 2. Where Caven Died: the Brenfen fen

*Map: Hags Den.*

The Brenfen fen, out on the peat flats under the open sky, with a rundown cottage at its
heart. Caven grew up on its edge, a fen warden's son. The fen takes bodies slowly and gives
them back wrong. This is where Caven died, and where he pulled himself back from between the
planes.

**Why Caven thinks she went there:** Raven asked about this place more than anything else
about him. Where exactly it happened, what the ground was like, what he saw. Every time they
got stuck, she came back to it. To her it was not where he died. It was where something
*worked*: a man who came back on his own, without a ritual or a plan. If she ever needed to
see how it was done, she would start where it happened. And Raven did not like to work around
people. A ruined cottage in a swamp no one visits is exactly where she would set up.

**What he feels:** he has never wanted to go back. Whatever left him there might still be
there.

---

## 3. Deep in the Ruins under Ulearos

*Maps: Demonically Tainted Hamlet of Grimsby (the road in), Fairfield Market (the town at the
mountain's foot).*

Under Lone Peak, beneath the fallen flying city the goliaths live on, are ruins nobody has
seen for centuries. Deep inside, Caven once found a sealed door carved in the Ancient script.
Behind it are the Anchored: Ancient souls bound to this world, long past the point where they
were still people.

**Why Caven thinks she went there:** he found them first. He did not understand them, and he
gave up. But he brought Raven a rubbing of the glyphs and told her everything he knew: that
these were souls *held* here, kept from going wherever souls go, for thousands of years. That
is the question at the heart of his problem: what ties a soul to the world, and what happens
when it can't leave. He walked away from that door. Raven would not have. She kept the
rubbing.

**The way in:** the only road to the ruins runs through the towns at the foot of the mountain.
If she went this way, someone there saw her pass.

**For Talo:** this is his mountain. Souls kept underground and cut off from the sky are the
worst thing a Groundstruck can imagine.

---

## Which one first?

Caven can argue for all three, and none of them is certain. Each one is somewhere Raven
*would* go, not somewhere she has *been*. Let the party choose. Whatever they find at one can
point them to the next.
