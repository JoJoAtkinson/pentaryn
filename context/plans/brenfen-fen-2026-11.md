# Brenfen Fen — the orcs and Caven's cottage

**Read this when:** building the surface leg where the party crosses the fen to Caven's home: the
peaceful orc camp on the way, and the fen warden's cottage. **Design notes, nothing built yet.**
Live state (scene ids, who is where) stays in [`../pentaryn.md`](../pentaryn.md).

Joe's brief (2026-11-19): the party is heading into the swamps to Caven's home. The Slain Town is
now a night scene, so before the fen the party deals with the orcs: a *peaceful* orc tribe at a camp
site. Then Caven's hut. Gather the options first, build later.

## 1. What the story already fixes

- **Caven's home is the fen warden's cottage in the Brenfen Fen.** Brenfen is a peat causeway town
  east of the great lake, one of a dozen rival city-states in 3200 A.F. (`world/factions/ardenhaven/locations/brenfen.md`).
  Every parish keeps a fen warden who walks the bog at dawn and after storms, stakes or burns what the
  peat returns, and keeps "the book of who went in and when". Caven is a warden's son who "learned his
  letters from the ledger of the drowned".
- **His own words** (journal page "Finding Raven", in `Caven Calderon's Journal`, player-visible):
  "The peat flats where I grew up, a fen warden's son, with a rundown cottage out among the reeds. The
  fen takes bodies slowly and gives them back wrong. It is where I died, and where I pulled myself back
  from between the planes… My father's book still has my name in it, the night I went in… I never
  wanted to go back." The fen is lead 1 of 3 for finding Raven (then the Amethyst Dragon's Cave, then
  the ruins under Stonewatch). Scene folders `1. Dragon Cave`, `2. Swamp & Cottage`, `3. Slain Town`
  follow that list.
- **GM only:** Caven died out on the fen and summoned himself back; he later becomes Emperor Quintus
  (`world/factions/calderon-imperium/npcs/quintus-calderon.md`, "Before the throne"). Raven (the
  Elderholt Deep-Witch, now a lich, `world/factions/elderholt/elders/raven-the-vital-ascendant.md`)
  is missing and he believes she went to these three places ahead of him. Nothing player-facing says
  any of this.
- **Things to put in the cottage** that the text already promises: the father's **book of the drowned**
  with Caven's name and the date he went in; the stakes, lye and burning-irons of the warden's trade;
  a reason to believe Raven was here first.

## 2. The orcs: who they should be

Bazgar (PC, Orc Fighter 6, `ep1NnwjAhSB74iP8`) is **Dakmar'nak**, the Rakthok Horde's marsh tribe
(`world/factions/rakthok-horde/tribes/dakmarnak.md`, `…/locations/black-marsh.md`). That makes the
peaceful tribe his own people or their kin, which is the easiest reason for the camp to be friendly:

- Engineers and spirit-keepers, literate, elected Fire Council of five elders, a war-leader who can be
  recalled. They school their children in spirit law, geography and enemy customs.
- **Blackened iron** forged with alligator fat and peat charcoal, so it does not rust in the marsh.
  **Cold-water alligators**, warm-blooded and semi-magical, for hides and sacred offerings.
- Black Marsh look: black-mud fen, mist, cypress islands, reed beds, mangroves, slow coal-dark
  channels, offerings on every hummock, Fire Council elections held on stone plinths in the marsh,
  Ancestor Fens where trespassers drown in ritual vines, iron-birch sap as lacquer.
- Motto "Guard the spirit, claim the soul." Colours obsidian, dawn copper, spirit turquoise. Twin
  spirit-flames. Outsiders think them raiders; they think the Imperium harvests souls.
- Naming: `world/factions/rakthok-horde/orc-clan-naming.md` (marsh clans end in `-mar` / `-mog`,
  "those who endure" `-nak`).
- ⚠ Era: the Horde's written history is 4200s A.F. and the one-shot is 3200 A.F. Joe's call whether
  these are proto-Dakmar'nak or the dates slide. Do not write a date into anything built.

**A camp that says "peaceful" at a glance:** children and elders visible, a cook fire not a war
drum, racks of eel and alligator hide, a shaman's plinth with offerings, canoes, no palisade of
skulls. Hostile-looking art (skull lanterns, cave camp) is the wrong signal.

## 4. Tokens

**No 2024 orc stat block exists.** The Monster Manual 2024 has none (orcs are a species now); the 2014
SRD pack `dnd5e.monsters` has only `Orc` (CR 1/2). So the orcs are **generic MM 2024 blocks reskinned**:

| Role | Block (`dnd-monster-manual.actors`) | CR |
|---|---|---|
| civilians, children, elders | Commoner | 0 |
| hunters, fishers, scouts on the boardwalks | Scout | 1/2 |
| rank-and-file warriors | Warrior Infantry | 1/8 |
| the camp's best, a Fire Council guard | Warrior Veteran | 3 |
| berserk champion | Berserker | 2 |
| shaman | Druid (or Priest for an ancestor-speaker) | 2 |
| war-leader | Warrior Commander | 10 (too high for level 5–6 PCs; cut it down or use Berserker Commander 8 as a non-fight) |

**Art on disk** (`Data/assets/tokens/tokens-01/orcs-and-half-orcs/`, round bordered PNGs; a ring
subject must be built with `scripts/foundry/ring_subject.py` for each one used). The ones that read
as **civilians**: `orc-cook`, `orc-druid-solemn`, `orc-druid`, `orc-swashbuckler` (bare-chested
dockhand), `half-orc-hunter`, `orc-ranger`, `orc-blue` (elder), `orc-mage` (a schooled Dakmar'nak).
**Warriors**: `orc-warrior`, `orc-fighter`, `determined-orc-fighter`, `orc-berserker`, `orc-barbarian-with-giant-axe`,
`orc-warrior-on-snow`, `orc-battle-driver`, `orc-chieftain` and `orc-warlord` for the leader,
`orc-paladin` for a Veteran. Avoid `raging-orc` (gore) and `orc-rider` (cavalry) for a peaceful camp.
Ring subjects already exist for `orc-warrior`, `orc-cook`, `orc-berserker`, `half-orc-hunter`,
`orc-fighter`, `half-orc-with-tattoos` (`Data/assets/tokens/custom/ring-subjects/`). `orc-cook` is
already Ursk (Inmates) and Grosh, so pick something else for a named cook.

**Other sources:** `modules/dnd-players-handbook/assets/tokens/orc-01…04.webp` (the 2024 PHB species
art: a falconer, a halberdier, a cowboy; friendly, modern, ⅔ safe-area already, four only).
`systems/dnd5e/tokens/humanoid/Orc.webp` is the top-down SRD token, a different style.
**Crowd:** there is no orc crowd set like `generic-villagers/`. A camp of twenty needs either a faded
crowd build from the PHB four and the civilian PNGs (the ring-less crowd convention,
[`../foundry/README.md`](../foundry/README.md) Token conventions) or FA Nexus "orc" tokens, not
checked.
