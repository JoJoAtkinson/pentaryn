# Drag movement (pentaryn-movement)

**Read this when:** a dragged token takes a strange route or will not go round a wall; several tokens dragged together overlap, jam in a door, or end up on top of each other; a moving token will not stop when clicked, or stops in the wrong place; the movement spent in a fight looks wrong after a stop; or a group drag "did nothing for a moment". **Not this file:** ghost movement and owned-on-top, the module's code, settings table and API → [`../../foundry/module/pentaryn-movement/README.md`](../../foundry/module/pentaryn-movement/README.md) · how it was designed and the live play-test list → [`../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md`](../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md) (§9, §12, §13).

## At the table

There is nothing to learn: drag, and it works out the rest.

1. **One token past a wall.** The ruler goes around it the shortest way (open doors only, round hostile creatures and costlier terrain, a big token only where it fits), corners rounded where that costs nothing. A player's drag only routes round walls that player has seen, and only over ground that player has explored or can see. A route never uses a secret door, open or shut, and never crosses a region with a Teleport Token (a pentaryn-portals scene link) or Change Level behaviour unless the drag ends (or starts) inside it; a hidden region counts only for the GM. A straight drag is core's own, except that it too bends round such a region rather than walk into it (0.4.5, live R5-4: the token was teleported and the player's view jumped).
2. **Several tokens at once** (box-select or Shift-click, then drag one). The one you grab lands exactly where you drop it (dropped on another token, on the nearest clear spot it can reach, 0.4.8). The others each walk their own way to their own spot around it, out of combat round any token standing in the way (0.4.8). On open ground they all set off together and keep their shape; only where routes meet (a door) do they set off in turn and file through one behind the other. No group spot is ever inside a scene-link region unless the group is dropped into it. At rest no two overlap (tokens are circles: touching is fine). While dragging, only the grabbed token's ruler bends; the others' routes are worked out on the drop.
3. **A long detour you did not want.** Click the moving token: it stops on the nearest free whole square (never across a door's line or a wall, never on another token) and is selected (0.4.9, Joe's decision 2). A click on one member stops the whole group (0.4.9, decision 3; 0.4.8 stopped only the clicked one, live H1-05); when the clicker does not own every member (a player's token in the GM's group), only the clicker's own members stop, and the client that sent the group walks the others round them, or stops them short of it where there is no way round that is not a long detour (H2-02). A player's own members stopped while the GM's walk on keep out of a one-wide corridor or doorway when there is a free square in the open within 3 squares (0.4.10, live H3-05). In a fight it stops at the next square instead, so dnd5e's spent movement is exactly the squares walked; two members that finish on one square are set apart once at rest, the later one displaced to the nearest free square (0.4.10, live H3-01). A member whose wave was still on its way to the server when the group was clicked stops as soon as it moves (0.4.10, live H3-05). The click only stops a move that has run 400 ms: a click right after the drop, or a double click to open the sheet, is an ordinary click. A click on a moving token over a door's icon, or on the icon with the token right beside it (within 0.4 square, 0.4.7), stops the token and leaves the door alone (0.4.5; before, it shut the door on it, live R5-3); a click on the icon with nothing moving under it toggles the door as usual.
4. **The GM's drag-help button** (Token controls, the route icon; world setting `assist`, 0.4.9): one click turns pathfinding and group movement off for the whole table, every drag then plain Foundry dragging; click again to bring them back. Click to stop keeps working.
5. **Ghost on, or the GM's Unconstrained Movement tool:** straight lines through walls, the group keeping its shape (only a token that would rest on top of another is moved aside).

## Out of combat vs in a fight

| | Out of combat | In a started combat (the token's combatant) |
|---|---|---|
| Path | free-form and smooth, arcs at corners | on the grid: no arcs, a checkpoint at every square |
| Group spots | packed round the drop point (a hex pattern) | whole squares round the drop point |
| Group timing | start delays, then a re-route round resting members if needed | start delays only |
| Click to stop | put on the nearest free whole square (a displace: free, recorded nowhere; 0.4.9), never across a wall or a door's line, never on another token or on a square another member of the same stop took | stops at the next square (`stopMovement`); never a displace |
| A player stopping a move the GM started | works (any owner may) | asked of the active GM, which stops it only when it started the move; on no answer in 400 ms nothing happens |
| The GM stopping a move a player started | works | asked of that player's client (`pentaryn-movement.stopOwn`, GM sender only), which stops it at the next square; on no answer in 400 ms, or the player offline, nothing happens |

## When something looks wrong

- **Read what the last drop did:** `game.pentaryn.movement.status().group.last` — `mode` (`plan` / `straight` / `core`), `reason` (why it went to core: `single`, `off`, `tooMany` (more than 12), `grid`, `direct`, `planned`, `waypoints` (Ctrl-click waypoints in a group drag), `pathfind:<why>`, `budget`, `anchorNoRoute`, `throw` (then `mode` and `result` are `core` too), `still` (dropped where it stands: nothing planned)), `waves`, `relaxed` (members allowed to brush while moving), `async`. `status().group.fallbacks` keeps the last few core fallbacks; `status().group.resumed` says when a reload last picked up unsent waves.
- **Dry run a group** from a browser console (the headless GM has no canvas): `game.pentaryn.movement.planGroup(["Talo", "Caven"], { x: 1200, y: 800 })` — the first is the grabbed one, `{ x, y }` its top-left drop point. Each member's `slot`, `delay` (ms) and `level` (0 a delay, 1 a re-route, 2 waited until the others stopped, 3 relaxed, `core` no route: it moves as core would). `anchorRoute` is the grabbed token's own route (searched first, as the drag preview does): `reached: false` there means no way to that point at all, not a planning budget.
- **"It paused, then they all moved":** a big group's plan did not finish in 30 ms, so the drop returned nothing and the moves went out when the plan was ready. The cap is the plan's own work, 1.2 s, or 2.5 s on the wall clock (0.4.8; it was 300 ms of wall clock, and a slow tab, drawing 100-400 ms frames between the plan's slices, fell back on most funnels, live H1-03). Past it, core's own drop: followers then stop at the first wall, as core does, but each stops short of any member already resting there, so none ends on top of another.
- **"No route there"** warning, to the dragger only (0.4.9, Joe's decision 7; 0.4.8 said "No way there", live H1-12): out of combat there is no way to the drop at all (dropped in the rock, behind a shut door). Nothing moves, a single token neither. The drag's own path is checked by one more search first, since it can be short only because its search was still running at the drop (H2-12); then the drop takes the found route and says nothing. When that search has not finished either (a long maze route released quickly, live H3-04), the drop sends nothing at once and the token goes when the search is done, or stays with the warning; `status().group.last.async` is true then. A single drop goes where the pointer came up, not where core's drag last had it (0.4.10, live H3-13: 1-3 squares short). `status().group.last.reason` is `noRoute` (single) or `anchorNoRoute` (group).
- **A Large (or bigger) token stopped before a narrow door:** core tests walls at the token's centre only, so before 0.4.8 a 2×2 token walked through a one-square door with its body in the rock (live H1-04). Its drag goes round by a way it fits through when there is one, however long (decision 8); else it stops before the gap with its whole body clear of the frame (0.4.9, H2-11), the dragger is warned "does not fit through there", and the GMs get a whisper when a player dragged it. Use ghost to pass it through by hand when it does fit.
- **"The ruler went straight through a wall and the token stopped at it"** (0.4.1 and before, in a maze whose opening was far off the straight line): the search looked only near the start and the drop. From 0.4.2 it searches the whole scene when that finds nothing.
- **A group member took a marathon round the dungeon** (live H3-03, fixed 0.4.10): a re-plan round the others' rest spots, or round tokens standing in the way, is now refused past 4 squares or half the direct way longer; the member waits its turn for the door instead.
- **"The route went the long way round":** the short way crossed a secret door, a scene-link region, or (for a player) unexplored ground. That is on purpose. Drag in a straight line through it if you mean to go there.
- **"I clicked it and it kept going":** the move had run under 400 ms (the arming delay); click again. A click lands on where the token is drawn now, even when core's own click handling misses a token that moved under the pointer.
- **A door was shut while the group walked through it:** members already through carry on; the ones still waiting each stop on their own whole square before the door, the nearest one free of every other token and of the squares the members before them took (0.4.4; 0.4.2 and 0.4.3 sent them all to the same square, live R4-1). A member with no free square on its way stays where it stands. A member already in the doorway walks through, as a single token does. From 0.4.5 the square where core will stop a member that is still walking with waypoints to go counts as taken too (live R5-1), and once the group is at rest a member left on top of another steps to the nearest free whole square (a walk; a displace in a fight); `status().group.destacked` says when that last happened.
- **A door cut drew a token through the shut door, then snapped it back, or left it frozen in the door frame with core's TypeError "reading 'chain'"** (live H1-01 / H1-02; fixed 0.4.8): on a slow tab core registered the wrong tween of a chained walk, so a stop missed the one still running. A token stopped with its document just re-prepared also jumped to its destination first. Both fixed; a member cut in the doorway with no square before the door walks to the nearest clear spot nobody else takes, and in a fight one that core stops on another's square steps off at once (H1-07), not 4 s later.
- **A door shut, or a wall raised, while tokens were already walking toward it** (0.4.6; before, out of combat they walked straight through it, live R6-3, and in a fight they all stopped on the same last square and were split with one-square jumps 4 s later, live R6-1): each stops on its own whole square before the wall, the nearest to it choosing first, free of every other token. Out of combat it stops at once where it is drawn, on every screen, and walks on to that square (0.4.7; 0.4.6 drew the old walk on through the door first, then jumped it back, live R7-1); in a fight it finishes the square it is in and walks on, costed. Only the client that started a move cuts it. `status().group.cut` says when that last happened. A Wall of Stone raised across a route cuts it the same way.
- **A member walked straight into a wall and stopped:** it had no route to any spot; it is left to core, and the others plan around where core leaves it.
- **Overlap while moving on another screen:** each client starts a tween when the update arrives, so a late wave can drift by the network delay. Members are kept 0.25 square further apart across waves for that; a region that holds a member at a checkpoint can still bring two together (accepted).
- **Next Turn while a group walks (in a fight):** the whole group stops at its next squares and the members still waiting stay put (0.4.4; before, only the members that had walked a square stopped, live R4-3). Core stops any token whose movement history is cleared, and a turn start clears every combatant's history, so what they walked belonged to the turn that ended, as for a single token. `status().group.stopped` says when a stop last stopped a group: `why` is `turnChange`, `click` (a click or `stop()`, 0.4.10) or `gm` (a GM's stop of a player's group in a fight). Drag again for the new turn.
- **Reloading the page while a group walks:** the members still waiting for their turn set off once the page is back (0.4.3), as long as nobody moved them meanwhile and it is within a minute of the drop. A wave due while the page is unloading is kept for the reloaded page, and a wave leaves the tab's record only once the server has it (0.4.5; 0.4.4 lost the last wave, often the grabbed token's, when the old page sent it over a closing socket, live R5-2). Only the tab that dropped them sends them (its own sessionStorage); closing that tab for good leaves them where they stand.
- **Clicked it, it stopped, and the drawn token walked on anyway** (0.4.2 and before, through a door): the server had it where it was clicked. From 0.4.3 every client watches a stopped token for 3 s and puts a token that walks on, or rests anywhere else, back where the server has it.
- **Undo (Ctrl+Z) after a group move** undoes one wave at a time (each wave is its own update).
- **Turn things off:** GM, the drag-help button in Token controls (all of it, for everyone, 0.4.9), or *Settings → Pentaryn Movement → Group movement* (world). Each player, *Click a moving token to stop it*, *Find a path when I drag*, *Round path corners* (client). `game.pentaryn.movement.stop(token)` stops one from the console.
- **Force the fallback** (live test 11): `CONFIG.debug["pentaryn-movement.throw"] = true`, drag a group (one warning, core's move), then delete the key.
