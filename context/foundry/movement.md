# Drag movement (pentaryn-movement)

**Read this when:** a dragged token takes a strange route or will not go round a wall; several tokens dragged together overlap, jam in a door, or end up on top of each other; a moving token will not stop when clicked, or stops in the wrong place; the movement spent in a fight looks wrong after a stop; a group drag leaves someone behind; or the drag help switched itself off. **Not this file:** ghost movement and owned-on-top, the module's code, settings table and API → [`../../foundry/module/pentaryn-movement/README.md`](../../foundry/module/pentaryn-movement/README.md) · how it was designed, the acceptance criteria and the live play-test list → [`../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md`](../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md) (§16 for 0.8, §17 for 0.9, §18 for 0.10).

> **0.10.2 is built on the branch `movement-0.9` (the world runs it once deployed; 0.10.1 before). 0.10.1 was live and built on the branch `movement-0.9`** (the leader rule, march and swarm, any passage shape; Fable's reviews 2 and 3); `main` keeps 0.8.5 (tag `movement-0.8.5-rollback`) until Joe has judged the hands-on ([`../plans/movement-0.10-handoff.md`](../plans/movement-0.10-handoff.md)). This page describes 0.10; where 0.9.0 differed it says so.

## The one rule

**Within 4 seconds of the drop every token is on its way, or named in a notice; nothing ever waits for another mover.** Hard (never broken): walls, shut doors, tokens standing still, a mate at its spot, two tokens overlapping at rest, and a fight's movement charges. Soft (allowed, Joe 2026-11-24): two tokens that are *both moving* may brush or overlap briefly on the way (the simulator measures it: about a square deep at worst, under 2 s at p95).

## At the table

There is nothing to learn: drag, and it works out the rest. Two world settings: `pathfinding` (Everyone / GM only / Off; each player also has *Find a path when I drag*) and `groupDrags` (on). (0.10 removed 0.9.0's `groupStagger`, B1 against B0: the motion is the leader rule's.) The GM's drag-help button (Token controls, the route icon; `assist`) turns both off for the whole table in one click. Up to 24 tokens in one group drag (0.10; was 12).

1. **One token past a wall.** The ruler goes around it the shortest way (open doors only, round tokens standing in the way and costlier terrain, a big token only where it fits), corners rounded where that costs nothing. A player's drag only routes round walls that player has seen, and only over ground that player has explored or can see. A route never uses a secret door and never crosses a region with a Teleport Token (a pentaryn-portals scene link) or Change Level behaviour unless the drag ends or starts inside it. No way there at all: nothing moves, "No route there." What the ruler shows is what the drop walks and charges, and it goes at once: no queue, no start delay, no waiting for another mover.
1b. **Any passage shape** (0.10; no switch of its own — the drag-help button stays the only off switch). A way narrower than the token still passes when its centre can keep **0.15 square (9 in)** from both walls: a way of 0.3 square (1.5 ft) or more. A curved stair (the ZZ Playground's S05 spiral C), a diagonal slit, a funnel, a hairpin, a field of pillars: one drag crosses in one smooth motion **on the way's centre line**. Narrower than 0.3: "No route there", or the long way round. Tokens up to 1×1 only: a Large takes the long way round or stops before the mouth (decision 8). Nothing costs more for being narrow (only a Difficult Terrain region the GM paints). Dropped or stopped part-way it rests off the grid on the centre line, in a fight too (there the walk is on the grid except through the narrow way, a checkpoint where its centre line enters each square, charged what core measures). Dropped in the rock beside the way, or off its line, it rests on the nearest centre-line point (the ruler shows the snapped square as not reached; Shift-drop for an exact spot). A group goes leader first and files behind; dropped on the narrow way, or just past its mouth, the members rest behind the leader on its centre line, a token apart (a point back along the leader's way counts as a spot whenever it is nearer the leader than a square behind, or a member has no square at all; in a fight too). A group's plan never ranks a square across an off-grid wall. Only squares an off-grid wall runs through are touched: a map with walls on the grid lines routes as before. `pathfind.route(token, { to })` reports `narrow` and `minClearance` (squares) and `goal` (where a drop in a tight square was moved to).
2. **Several tokens out of combat — the leader rule** (0.10) (box-select or Shift-click, then drag one). **The one you grab leads** (grab a rider and its mount leads). Where you drop it picks the shape. **March** — dropped on the floor: the leader ends on the drop square by its own way (walking through its own group, never through anyone else; on a hidden token or where it cannot stand: the nearest free square), and everyone else rests behind it, as close as they fit, never beside or ahead of it ("behind" follows the leader's last step, not the straight drag line); a one-wide tunnel gives a line behind the leader. **Swarm** — dropped on a token outside the group that you can see (friend, foe or prop): the leader gets a square touching it first, and the rest close in round it from their own sides, ring by ring (8 squares touch a Medium, 12 a Large, 16 a Huge); through a doorway they file in, then fan out. A hidden token never makes a swarm, for the GM either. Each member takes the best square it can reach and gets one route to it, round the tokens standing outside the drag and the squares already given out. **Then the motion** (0.10 L2): the leader sets off in the drop's own update, drawn on top of everyone on every screen while it walks (through its own members, if it starts behind them); the rest set off one wave (150 ms, about a square) later as one pack, or, when the leader starts behind, once it is a wave past the frontmost member. Single file only where the way is narrow (a door: one through at a time; a one-wide way: a line), spreading out again past it; a member also waits while another would walk through it at rest. Everyone is on their way within 4 s. One with no square it can reach stays where it stands and is named ("Stayed where they stand, with no free spot they can reach: …"); the leader with no way moves nobody: "No route there." A tab sent to the background keeps the waiting members in order (its timers fire on whole seconds; the last still goes within 4 s). `planGroup(tokens, point)` dry-runs it (`shape`, `leader`, `target`, each member's square, route, delay, stay).
3. **Several tokens in a fight — one at a time.** Nothing moves at the drop. The leader walks first (to the drop square, or the nearest clear one), then the others one after the other (least walk left first; a mount and its rider as one), each a normal single move whose square is picked when its turn comes (behind where the leader came to rest in a march, round the target in a swarm) and routed round every token standing *then* (those already moved where they came to rest), a checkpoint at every square, charged exactly what it walks (5 ft a square). The leader walks straight through its own group (never through anyone else) and pays nothing extra for it (2024 rules); its moves end only on free squares, so a click while it is inside a member carries it on to the next free one. **A member whose speed runs out stops** on the last free square its movement this turn pays for: dnd5e's own reading, the walking speed (or the movement action's, with its walk fallback) less what the token's movement history already cost this turn; with nothing left it does not move. Both are named: "Out of movement this turn, stopped short: …". No speed on the sheet: the whole way, as a single drag does (single drags are unchanged). `status().group.queue.walks` has each member's planned and sent squares and the movement it had left. The leader with no way moves nobody ("No route there"). Expect about a second per member per 30 ft. A click on the walker, a turn change or a deleted member ends the queue; the members not yet sent are named ("Did not move, the group's walk was ended: …") unless your own click ended it. A new drop of other tokens leaves it running (0.10); one of its members dropped again leaves the queue. A walk that never ends is moved on after its own time plus 3 s. More than 24 members: "In a fight, drag one token at a time."
4. **Click a moving token to stop it** (always on; a move you dropped: once it has moved 400 ms; one someone else set moving: at once). **A click on any member of a moving group stops the whole group**: on the dragger's screen every member and every wave not yet sent (a member whose wave was already on its way stops where it is drawn the moment its move comes back); from another screen the one clicked, and the dragger's screen then stops the rest. Out of combat each stops **exactly where it is drawn** (Joe, 2026-11-23); one with its body across a door's line, or that would rest inside a token at rest (a mate the same stop stopped, a spot this screen just sent a mate to, a spot another stop on this screen is carrying a token on to), is carried on along its own route just clear of it, never through a wall or a token at rest (the leader of a group carries on through its own members to the first free spot). Several stopped at once settle front first (0.10 L3): the one in front where it is drawn, the ones behind it carried back along their own ways (or, when one behind has nowhere, the ones it lies in carried on along theirs to make room); never onto the square of a member still waiting for its wave; and this screen's other walkers whose way comes into a spot the stop rests a token on are stopped by the same stop. A carry whose way a door shut since crosses (a click just after a door shut, before this screen knew) takes its spot again along its way. With no clear spot anywhere along its own way (0.10: no straight line off it any more) a click leaves it walking on to its own square, named ("Could not stop clear of another token, so walked on to its own square: …"); a door or wall that cut it leaves it where it is drawn, named ("Stopped on another token, …: drag it clear"). In a fight it finishes the square it is in, so the movement spent stays exact. A GM may stop anyone's move; a player the moves of tokens they own.
5. **A door shuts or a wall rises while tokens walk.** Out of combat each token whose way it crosses stops where it is drawn, and a member not yet sent whose way it crosses stays where it stands, named. A door is not shut on a jam (0.10 L3): when one of the tokens walking through it would have no spot clear of every token along its own way, the shut is refused ("Door blocked: <name> is in the doorway"); shut it once they are through. In a fight every next square is tested against the walls *as they are now* before it is sent (a door shut mid-walk stops the token on the square it holds, charged for the squares it walked); no square is sent from a page that no longer shows the token's map, none into a token standing still, and (0.10 L3) none onto the square of a walker that rests there or whose screen has held it up for half a second.
6. **Stacked tokens stay stacked**: a rider on its mount, a familiar on a shoulder, a grappled pair (circles that start overlapping) walk their leader's way at their offset. A mount dragged alone carries its rider; a rider dragged alone leaves its mount.
7. **Ghost on, or the GM's Unconstrained Movement tool:** straight lines through walls, the group keeping its shape (core's own move). Ctrl waypoints and one stack are core's own moves too.

**Two notices only for a move:** "No route there." (nothing moves) and "Stopped short, way blocked." (it moved as far as it could). Why, exactly, is a reason code in `game.pentaryn.movement.telemetry()`.

**Fail-open and the circuit breaker.** An exception inside a move sends core's own move (cut before standing tokens) and is logged. After 3 errors or 6 failed plans in one session the drag help switches itself off on that screen; on the GM's screen it also turns `assist` off for the table, and the GM gets a notice (a whisper when it was a player's screen). A reload, or the GM turning the button back on, clears it.

**What a reload does:** nothing resumes. A token mid-walk when its page reloads stays on the square it holds; the group members not yet sent stay where they stand. Drag again.

**"That move was wrong."** Press **Alt+W** (rebindable in Configure Controls) right after a bad move: the last ten seconds of that screen's drag telemetry are filed as a Bug through pentaryn-feedback, for `feedback_next`.

## Out of combat vs in a fight

| | Out of combat | In a started combat (the token's combatant) |
|---|---|---|
| Path | free-form and smooth, arcs at corners | on the grid: no arcs, a checkpoint at every square |
| Group drag | the leader rule: every member's spot and route at the drop, the leader first and the pack a wave later, all within 4 s | the leader first, then one at a time (least walk left), each slotted and routed at its turn, stopping where its speed runs out |
| Tokens standing in the way | routes go round them; none rests on one; no way round: stops short | the same, on whole squares; the ruler already shows that way and its cost |
| Two movers meeting | may brush (soft) | one walks at a time, so never |
| Click to stop | the whole group, each exactly where drawn (a displace: free, recorded nowhere); across a door's line, carried just past it | the whole group's queue ends; the walker stops at the next square (`stopMovement`); never a displace |
| Door shut mid-walk | each whose way it crosses stops where drawn | the walker stops on the square it holds |
| The dragger views another scene mid-walk | core sends the rest unconstrained from a page that no longer draws the token: not across a wall that blocks movement now (read from the scene's walls) | the next square is not sent, the token stops on the square it holds |
| A player stopping a move the GM started | works (any owner may) | asked of the active GM, which stops it only when it started the move; no answer in 400 ms: nothing happens |
| The GM stopping a move a player started | works | asked of that player's client (`pentaryn-movement.stopOwn`, GM sender only); no answer in 400 ms: nothing happens |

## When something looks wrong

- **Start with the telemetry** on the screen that moved the token: `game.pentaryn.movement.telemetry({ seconds: 30 })` — `summary.counts`, `summary.fallbacks` and `summary.notices` by reason code, `tripped` (the breaker), and the events themselves (counters named for 0.8's guard and reservations stay at zero). `game.pentaryn.movement.reportTelemetry()` whispers the summary to the GMs.
- **Read what the last drop did:** `status().group.last` — `mode`, `reason` (why it went to core: `single`, `off`, `tooMany` (more than 24), `grid`, `direct`, `waypoints`, `pathfind:<why>`, `throw`, …), `shape`, `leader`, `waves`, `stayed`, `plan` (`total`: the last start, `pack`: the pack's, `crowded`, `timingMs`, `searches`, `failed`). `status().group.leading` lists the leaders drawn on top now. `status().group` also has `pending` (waves not yet sent), `wave` (the last later wave), `flushed` (the last time the tab went to the background with waves to send: `kept`, `retimed`) and `queue` (the last fight queue: members, sent, stayed, skipped, watchdog, ended, running). `status().group.told` lists the last notices.
- **Dry run a group** from a browser console: `game.pentaryn.movement.planGroup(["Talo", "Caven"], { x: 1200, y: 800 })` — the first is the grabbed one, `{ x, y }` its top-left drop point. It returns each member's spot, route, start `delay` and whether it stays; nothing moves.
- **Why did that member stay?** No free spot it can reach (behind a wall, off the scene, a one-wide corridor full of mates), or no route to it round the standing tokens: it is named in the notice, and `planGroup` shows `stay`.
- **"Every token froze for a moment":** the telemetry's `stall` events (frames over 150 ms while this screen moved tokens). Core's fog of war reading the explored texture back from the GPU stalls player tabs 150–650 ms (traced 2026-11-22); with the scene's fog mode off the walk is smooth. Not fixed here (core).
- **A Large (or bigger) token stopped before a narrow door:** its drag goes round by a way it fits through when there is one, however long; else it stops before the gap with its whole body clear of the frame, "Stopped short, way blocked", and the GMs get a whisper naming it when a player dragged it. Use ghost to pass it through by hand.
- **"The route went the long way round":** the short way crossed a secret door, a scene-link region, or (for a player) unexplored ground. On purpose. Or a narrow way is under 0.3 square somewhere (its centre cannot keep 0.15 from both walls), or the token is bigger than 1×1.
- **A narrow way looks wrong** (a stair, a slit): `pathfind.route(token, { to })` — `narrow`, `minClearance` (squares, at least 0.15), `goal`; `pathfind.status().last` the same for the last drag. A way that should pass but is refused is narrower than 0.3 square somewhere (measure the walls); the walls are read once per scene and again after any wall or door change.
- **A token stopped and nobody knows why:** `status().stop.last` is this client's last click-stop (`how`, `via`: `click`, `board`, `door`, `api`; `pastDoor` when it was carried past a door's line; `walkedOn` when one could not stop clear of a token at rest and walked on to its own square).
- **"Why did the others wait?"** `status().group.last.plan.pack` is when the pack set off (150 ms after the leader, more when the leader started behind them); a later delay is single file at a door or one-wide way, or a member holding back while another walks through its square. A wave the dragger's screen no longer draws (they changed scene) is not sent: its members are named.
- **Next Turn while a fight group walks:** the queue ends; the walker stops at its next square and the members still waiting stay put. They are named as not moved.
- **A fight's next square was not sent** ("Stopped short" mid-walk): `status().group.step` — `why` `standing` (a token standing still in its next square on this screen), `unrendered` (the dragger's page no longer draws the token: another scene), `wallUnrendered` (a wall shut across it).
- **"No route there: <name> is in the way."** A single drag whose only way passes a token standing in it (an ally in a one-wide corridor in a fight). Toggle ghost under the Ruler to pass it, or move it first. A name you cannot see reads "a token".
- **Undo (Ctrl+Z) after a move the module drove** undoes the whole move in one press: a group's waves, a fight's every square and the module's own writes for it are one undo entry; each token goes back where the move found it, with its movement history (and its charge) as it was. A macro's moves keep core's one entry per update.
- **A flying token** (movement action `fly`) passes over the tokens on the ground and they under it: no route goes round, nobody gives way.
- **Turn things off:** GM, the drag-help button (all of it, for everyone), or the `pathfinding` / `groupDrags` settings. Each player, *Find a path when I drag* (client). `game.pentaryn.movement.stop(token)` stops one from the console.
- **Force the fallbacks** (live checks): `CONFIG.debug["pentaryn-movement.throw"] = true` (a planner throw) or `CONFIG.debug["pentaryn-movement.throwDeliver"] = true` (a delivery throw): drag a group, core's move with one warning; delete the keys after.

## Known limits (0.10.2)

Accepted for Joe's hands-on; the backlog order is in [`../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md`](../plans/pathfinding-2026-11.md) §18.9.

- **A narrow way fuller than it holds** (the playground's S05 stair): members line up behind the leader on the way's centre line, a token or a little more apart; once the leader has gone through to the room beyond, one that finds no spot on the line stays where it stands and is named ("Stayed where they stand, with no free spot they can reach"); never left in the far room unnamed (0.10.1). Eight dragged back and forth over the stair all come through (S05.q). Their plan takes about 0.6 s of work (1.0 s on the clock, cold), in slices between frames (0.10.2-dev): the leader sets off at the drop, the rest as their waves come once it is done.
- **The door plug** (0.10.2): a leader dropped within about three squares past a one-wide door or mouth brings in as many as fit on the free squares within 2.5 squares of it, behind and beside it, never ahead of it; the square just inside the door fills last, and the rest wait outside, behind. Dropped one square past, the leader stands in the way in and nobody passes it (no diagonal squeeze past the door frame). Not on a narrow way's tight squares (the stair: the line behind) and not in a fight. Drag deeper into the room to bring everyone in. `status().group.last.plan.plug` (and `planGroup`'s `plug`): the walk past the door and the squares inside, null when none.
- **A fight walk on an off-grid way is charged core's measure**, 5 ft per square entered, not the distance walked (130 ft for 118 ft; 240 for 212 through mud; 30 for 25). Joe's call (R7 against R9); a stop part-way still rests off the grid where it is.
- **A Large leader that does not fit the way:** nobody moves, "No route there." (the pack does not form behind where it stops). A Large follower that cannot follow stays where it stands, named. A leader whose rest square is held by a member that stays: nobody moves, the notice names that member.
- **The door veto:** a door cannot be shut on a jammed group token with nowhere free to rest ("Door blocked: <name> is in the doorway"); a player who cannot see the token is told "a token".
- **Any gap of 1.5 ft or more between walls is a way** for Medium and smaller tokens, with no switch: a real map may hold a gap that was meant to be shut. A march crosses painted mud in a straight line (as 0.9.0 does; only a Difficult Terrain region the GM paints costs more, and a march out of combat is not charged).
- **Timing:** 0.10.1 (the world): the plan blocks the page 45 ms for 8 and 150 ms for 20 on ordinary ground (GPU page), 156 ms for twelve at a door, 0.7 to 1.0 s for eight over the stair; at 4x CPU throttle 20 takes about 0.8 s. 0.10.2: no page freeze. The drop plans for 16 ms; a plan not done by then goes on in 8 ms slices between frames (no task over 50 ms from the drop to the plan's end on the GPU page, the drop's own task 17-28 ms, under 55 ms at 4x throttle), the leader at once; its wall time is about 1.7 times its work (S05 eight cold 1.0 s for 0.56 s of work, a door's twelve 0.3 s, twenty on open ground 0.3 s), and a wave due before the plan ended goes when it ends. Frames can still be 50-150 ms apart while it runs (Foundry's own work on the moves' writes). Several tokens at one door (twelve) can be in the frame together because nothing may wait past 4 s; they overlap only while moving. Search caps: leader 1,000 ms, member 400 ms, plan 1,500 ms.
- **In a fight** `status().group.last.plan` carries members and order only. A swarm dropped on a narrow way keeps whole-square rings. A square crossed by two lanes of one area (a hairpin) is one square to the group plan.
- **Narrow ways are outside the simulator** (it runs on the square grid): unit tests and the playground suite cover them. The code is bigger than its targets (drag assist 5,736 lines against 4,200, narrow ways 1,294 against 280) and `stop.mjs` holds three stop rules the settle made redundant (`stopOrder`, `makeRoom`, the brush): consolidation is backlog.

## What went in 0.10 (against 0.9.0)

Formation offsets (everyone keeping its place against the others: the dragons sliding in parallel lines); `groupStagger` and B0 (everyone at once: the setting is deleted once by the active GM); `file-core`'s `candidatesFor`, rings, `walkRatio`, tries, the route-clearance test, the fixed-point passes, the three reorders and `waveWays`; `sequence-core.mjs` whole (`stacksOf` moved to `group-core`); in `file.mjs` and `drop.mjs` the flood per member, `fightSpots`, `routeAtTurn`'s spot list, `sendWave`'s cut-short book-keeping (a member is sent whole or stays, named) and the "leader stays" branches; the straight-line carry of a click-stop. The group cap went from 12 to 24. No house rule and no `dnd5e.determineOccupiedGridSpaceDifficult` hook: the leader's way through its own group costs nothing.

## The simulator

`make foundry-movement-sim` (`SEEDS=10000`, `SEED=N` one seed's record, `OPTS='{"combat":true}'`) runs the module's real cores (`file-core` planLead and staggerFile, `motion-core` clearAlong, fightWalk, stopOrder, spotsAlong and settleAlong, the pathfind-core A*) on virtual clients over a jittered, lossy network, one run a seed (0.10 L2: B0 is gone). **Kinds of seed** (0.10 L3, `test/sim/scenario.mjs` KINDS): 0.9's mix kept draw for draw (`legacy`, 40 %), swarms on a visible target of 1×1, 2×2 or 3×3 (`swarm`), drops on a hidden token (`hidden`, a march), a party queued in a one-wide tunnel with its leader at the back or the front (`tunnel`), crowds of 10 to 24 across a hall (`crowd`), and Joe's ZZ Playground stations on the square grid with their thin and one-way walls, doors, scenarios and drop points (`station`; `test/fixtures/stations.json`, made from the playground's `stations.json`; S05 and S06 are off-grid curves, the narrow-passage step's). Fights (all but `legacy`) give most members a walking speed and some movement already spent. `OPTS='{"mix":"legacy"}'` runs 0.9's mix only, `{"kind":"station","station":"S04","scenario":"back"}` one kind. A failing run prints its seed and first failure; `SEED=N` gives the events (plans, notices, stops, cuts). Hard: `passThrough`, `wallCross`, `wrongCharge` (also a group member walking past what its movement pays for; a leader's update through its members must have every square but the last inside one), `unsent` (not sent or named within 4 s; nothing held at the end), `restOverlap` (not a flagged two-screen race), `shape` (march: nobody rests beside or ahead of the leader, except the door plug's squares beside it, judged on their own; swarm: the leader touches the target when a touching square was free and reachable; `test/sim/shape.mjs`, the L1 gate's own), `errors`. Reported only: mover overlap (episodes, p95 depth and duration, square-seconds), a walk past a mate waiting for its wave or stopped short, a walk through a token that came to rest after it was sent, the leader walking through its own members (0.10 R6, allowed). The suite runs 60 seeds and the regression seeds (`test/sim/sim.test.mjs`; the regressions on the legacy mix). A stop's writes go as one update there too, and its tokens are settled together as stop.mjs does (0.10 L3 fix round). Results and the open seeds: plan §17.5 (0.9), §18 (0.10; §18.3 the fix round).

## The playground

The ZZ Playground is the test bed for all of this, in the world and in the repo. Everything on it is a ZZ document (ZZ actors, one scene, three macros, one journal), so nothing real is touched.

- **The scene:** `ZZ Playground`, 100 by 100 squares, no art, global light. Sixteen stations in a 4 by 4 grid (S01 open field, S02 arena with a target to swarm, S03 a one-wide tunnel, S04 twenty monsters in a tunnel, S05 the spiral C stair, S06 passage shapes, S07 doors, S08 an unexplored room, S09 a dead end, S10 a narrow bridge, S11 mud, S12 one-way walls, S13 a door too narrow for a Large, S14 a two-way lane, S15 a crowd behind a wall, S16 a fight yard). Each station has a drawn title and instruction, its own heroes (Player8A owns Hero 1-4, Large, Huge, Slow Hero, Fast Hero 1-3; Player8B owns Hero 5-8, Tiny, Horse, Rider) and named drop points. View it, do not activate it. The station table is `automation/browser-test/movement-playground/pg/stations.md`.
- **The reset:** the journal `ZZ Playground` links the macros (a click on a macro link runs it); the macro folder `ZZ Playground` holds `ZZ PG reset: paths (vision off)` (the default: token vision off, fog off), `ZZ PG reset: sight (heroes only)` (vision on, fog explored and wiped, sight on the player-owned heroes only) and `ZZ PG scenario...` (a dialog: station, scenario, mode). A reset puts every token of the chosen station back on its start square, restores doors, regions and scenario walls, deletes the scene's combats, clears movement history and removes stray tokens. Run it on a page that has a canvas: the headless GM page (`Claude`) has none, so use a real GM tab or the suite's harness.
- **Rebuilding:** `pg/gen.py` writes `stations.json`, `stations.md`, `build.js`, `reset.js`, `install.js` and `verify.js` from one description; `build.js` rebuilds the scene (it deletes only the scene named ZZ Playground), `install.js` the macros and the journal, `verify.js` (`run-verify.sh`) checks the geometry (556 checks on the last run). - **The suite:** `automation/browser-test/movement-playground/suite/`. `./harness/start.sh wsl` (software GL, the "state" path: positions, notices, charges, shapes) and `./harness/start.sh gpu` (the BOSS GPU Chrome over CDP, the timing path), then `./run.sh <round> [all | S03,S05] [state | gpu | both]` (`--only S05.q`, `--list`). 120 checks over the 16 stations (`checks.md`): HARD (zero tolerance), GATED (a number), REPORTED (measured).
