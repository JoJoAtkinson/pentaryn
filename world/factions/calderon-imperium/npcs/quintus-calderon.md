## Background

Quintus Calderon is a dynast — he did not seize the throne, he was born to it. The Calderon name built the Imperium's edict code and standing legions, and Quintus governs as the living extension of that institutional will. He is more interested in the machinery of empire holding together than in personal glory.

### Before the throne: Caven (3200 A.F., the Pentaryn one-shot)

GM only. Caven's player does not know he becomes Quintus.

- **Origin:** a fen warden's son from [[world/factions/ardenhaven/locations/brenfen|Brenfen]].
- **The death:** Caven died. On the other side, caught between planes, he **summoned
  himself** back. That is where his necromancy comes from: he saw the other side.
- **The cost:** ever since, he has felt like he is **puppeting his own body**, the same way he
  puppets his undead. Mind and body are separate, and he drives the body from somewhere else.
- **What he is looking for:**
  1. **Where** he is puppeting himself from. In effect, where his soul is now.
  2. **How** to restore himself to his body and be his full self again, as he was before he
     died.
- **Raven:** [[raven-the-vital-ascendant|Raven]] sought him out. She does not have his problem,
  but she understands how he feels: mind and body at odds. They explored magic together
  looking for answers, and they understood each other.
- **The Underdark:** this is why he went into the drow libraries. He wanted knowledge of the
  realms and the nature of undeath, and above all to learn whether a soul is still tied to an
  undead body. That would tell him how he himself exists. A drow house caught him there.
- **Raven, as far as he knows:** still whole. Her lich rite (3200) happened while he was in the
  cells, so he doesn't know about it. Marrith, the other PC from Elderholt, believes the same.
- **Is he alive? Deliberately open.** Nobody knows, Caven included, and the not knowing is the
  problem. What frightens him:
  - If he is dead, maybe he *can't* come back, whatever he finds.
  - Maybe keeping this body going is the very thing holding him back from a higher plane.
  - Or there is nothing higher, and a full death leaves him stuck wherever his soul is now.
  He wants to find out which. Don't settle it at the table.

## Role in the Imperium

- Final authority over all edicts; the High Edict Council advises, but his word is law
- Commands ultimate disposition of the legion structure through the Provincial Legates
- The Censorate reports directly to him on matters of internal security and arcane R&D

## Using Quintus in Play

- Unlikely to appear in person outside Ironstead; his presence would signal something unprecedented
- Most contact with the Imperium filters through Legates, Prefects, or the High Censor
- When the players finally reach him, the scene should feel like an audience with an institution, not a person
- He is not a villain. He is the shape that power takes when it believes it is correct.
