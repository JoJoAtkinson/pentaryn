As war gave way to contracts and coin, Elaerith announced the Age of Trade with a warning: commerce is conquest in softer robes; she authorized exchange only through sanctioned hands and sworn measures, so that no foreign bargain could seep past the outer ring uncounted, unnamed, or unmade at her word.
