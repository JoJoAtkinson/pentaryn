With stone roads spreading across the continents, Elaerith introduced the Age of Roads as an age of reaching hands and marching feet; she instructed Araethilion to treat every new causeway as a boundary in disguise, and to answer the world’s bridges with deeper wards, quieter routes, and stricter permission.
