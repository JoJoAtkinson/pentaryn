Observing that Sabriel’s devotion set her apart from all others, Elaerith appointed a single cleric, Aersiliel (AIR-sill-ee-EL), to remain at her side—one voice, one presence, sufficient in place of many—so that Sabriel might know fellowship without dilution, and remain perfectly attended within the grace that had already set her above the rest.
