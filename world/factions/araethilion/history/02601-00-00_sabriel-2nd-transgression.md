When Sabriel again misjudged her place, Elaerith intervened with necessary correction, tempered only by maternal mercy; thereafter, Elaerith decreed that the tower would want for nothing, so that Sabriel would never again be misled into believing fulfillment lay beyond what her mother had already perfected.
