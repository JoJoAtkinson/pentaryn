When continental war became the world’s habit, Elaerith named the Age of Conquest and framed it as the inevitable end of those who live by appetite and horizon; within Araethilion, she tightened the rings of court order, strengthened the Choir-Sentinels, and reminded her people that safety is obedience, not distance.
