When other peoples began to rebuild, Elaerith proclaimed the Age of Reclamation to Araethilion as a reminder that renewal is not freedom: it is discipline—work done within bounds, memory curated, and every “rediscovered” road treated as proof that the world always returns, in time, to what she already preserves.
