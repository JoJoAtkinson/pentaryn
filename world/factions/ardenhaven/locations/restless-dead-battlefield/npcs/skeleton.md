# Skeleton

**HP** 13 (2d8+4) **·** **AC** 13 (armor scraps) **·** **Speed** 30 ft. **·** **Vulnerable** bludgeoning **·** **Immune** poison damage; exhaustion, poisoned **·** **Darkvision** 60 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 9 **·** **CR** 1/4 (50 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

**Languages:** understands the languages it knew in life but cannot speak. It does not answer questions. It reacts to *tone*.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **Is it threatened?** A skeleton that has not been attacked, blocked, or grabbed does **not** fight — it takes **Shamble Onward** and keeps walking west toward Ardenford. Only a skeleton that has been damaged or physically stopped rolls attacks.
2. **Bludgeoning check:** if a PC hit it with a mace, hammer, staff, quarterstaff, sling, or a falling rock this round, that damage was **doubled**. Say so out loud — the table should learn it fast.
3. **The pauldron:** one of the four (DM's pick, usually the one at the front of the column) wears a corroded bronze pauldron with a faint oak-tree emblem. That one is **Corwin Caldor**. It behaves identically in combat; it just matters afterward.

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Default, every round, unless provoked:** Shamble Onward. It walks. It does not target anyone. It will step *around* a PC standing in its way rather than through them — once.
- **Round 1 if attacked:** the struck skeleton turns on whoever struck it and closes to shortsword range. Its neighbours do **not** join in unless they are also hit or blocked. This is important — the fight escalates one skeleton at a time, which is what makes walking away still possible.
- **Fights defensively, without strategy:** no flanking, no focus fire, no target priority. It attacks whatever is nearest and in front of it. If two PCs are equidistant, pick the one it was already facing.
- **Ranged:** a skeleton that is blocked but cannot reach anyone (PCs on the cairn rocks, on elevation, behind a gap) draws the shortbow. It will not step off high ground to chase.
- **If disengaged from:** it does not pursue. It reorients west and resumes walking. A party that backs off *ends the fight*.
- **Below 5 HP:** no fear, no retreat, no morale — it keeps swinging until it drops. Undead do not flee.
- **Gentle voices:** if a PC speaks calmly to it rather than swinging (see **Heed the Living**), it stops, turns its skull toward the voice, and waits. That's the moment the encounter can turn into something other than a fight.

## Description (one line)

A yellowed human skeleton in the rotted grey-and-oak livery of the Ridgeward Battalion, walking west with unhurried, purposeful steps, empty sockets fixed on something a hundred and twenty-seven years gone.
