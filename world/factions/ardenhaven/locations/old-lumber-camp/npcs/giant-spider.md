# Giant Spider

**HP** 26 (4d10+4) **·** **AC** 14 (natural armor) **·** **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft. **·** **Stealth** +7 **·** **Darkvision** 60 ft. **·** **Blindsight** 10 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 10 **·** **CR** 1 (200 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **Web** — if USED, roll 1d6 (`roll_dice(1, 6)`); it recharges on a 5 or 6.
2. **Spider Climb:** it moves at full speed on the palisade logs, the bunkhouse stone, the roof shakes, and the web lines slung between the timber stacks — including upside down. Track its height; it is almost never on the ground by choice.
3. **Web Sense:** it knows the position of anything touching its webbing, anywhere in the camp. Nobody sneaks up on it inside the palisade ring.
4. **Web Walker:** it ignores movement restrictions from webbing — its own or anyone's.
5. **Larder check:** has anyone cut down a cocoon or damaged an egg sac since its last turn? If yes, that creature is now its only target (see Tactics).

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Where it starts:** on the bunkhouse roof beside the warm chimney, or hanging in the rafters directly over the nest, depending on whether the party comes in loud. It does not come to the gate to meet them — it lets them walk into the ring first.
- **Round 1:** Web across the yard (60 ft range, DC 13 Dex save or Restrained). Priority target is whoever is closest to the **bunkhouse door** — the nest is the thing it is defending, not itself.
- **Follow up:** it crosses on the web lines from eaves to timber stack to palisade top, drops adjacent to a Restrained target, and Bites. Attacks against a Restrained creature have Advantage; take it.
- **It owns the vertical:** between bites it climbs back to the roof or the palisade crown, 10–15 ft up and out of shortsword reach. Melee PCs must climb the log stacks (10 ft of movement) or push it off the wall. Reward that.
- **Larder defence — the one hard rule:** the instant a PC cuts a cocoon down, opens one, or damages an egg sac, that PC becomes its target for the rest of the fight. Full stop. It will walk through opportunity attacks and ignore the fighter hitting it in the back to reach them. This is what turns a rescue into a real problem.
- **Indoors is a trade:** if it is fought inside the bunkhouse, its climb buys it almost nothing (12 ft to the rafters, stone walls, one door) — but the party is packed into a 20×30 ft room and a Web that lands is a disaster. It will happily drag a Restrained PC *toward* the doorway rather than away from it.
- **Fire:** it is not afraid of fire and has no instinct to save the nest from it. It does not flee a burning camp; it keeps biting while the webs go up.
- **No retreat:** a hunting spider does not run and does not parley. It fights until killed.

## Description (one line)

A grey, bristled hunter the size of a mastiff, folded against the bunkhouse chimney where the stone is warm, its legs bracketing a rope of web that runs from the eaves out to the top of the log wall.
