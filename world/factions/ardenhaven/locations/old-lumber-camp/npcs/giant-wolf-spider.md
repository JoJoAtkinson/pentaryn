# Giant Wolf Spider

**HP** 13 (3d6+3) **·** **AC** 13 (natural armor) **·** **Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft. **·** **Perception** +3 **·** **Stealth** +5 **·** **Darkvision** 60 ft. **·** **Blindsight** 10 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 13 **·** **CR** 1/4 (50 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **Spider Climb:** full-speed movement up the palisade logs, the timber stacks, and the bunkhouse walls, even upside down.
2. **No web of its own** — this is the fast ground hunter. Spend the whole 40 ft. every turn; the camp yard is only ~40 squares across and it can cross half of it and still bite.
3. **Where is it hiding?** Before initiative it is in a **sawpit**, under the plank walkway, or wedged in a gap in a log pile — Stealth +5 against passive Perception. Two of them do not hide in the same place.

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Ambush:** bursts out of a sawpit or from under a timber stack *behind* the party, after they've walked past it toward the bunkhouse. The gate is the bait; the yard is the trap.
- **Take the flank:** it works the opposite side from its partner. Two wolf spiders on either side of a PC is Advantage for both — they will do this without being told, every round they can.
- **Hit and get out:** fast enough to bite and still climb 15 ft up the palisade or a log stack, out of reach of anyone without a bow or a spear. Don't stand next to the fighter.
- **Guard the gate:** the cart gate is the only easy way out of the ring. Anything running for it gets chased down — 40 ft. against a PC's 30 ft. means the runner does not make the treeline.
- **Pit fighting:** it is entirely happy at the bottom of a sawpit and will drag a fight down there. A PC who falls in with one is prone, in the dark, with a spider that isn't.
- **Mindless:** no fear, no morale, no retreat — it fights to the death. It does not defend the nest the way the big one does; it just hunts whatever is moving nearest.

## Description (one line)

A low, mottled hunting spider the size of a large hound, the brown of wet sawdust, crouched in the shadow under the sawpit planks where the camp's own woodchips hide its outline.
