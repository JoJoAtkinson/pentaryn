# Brenfen

> Peat, eel-smoke and a council that would rather drown than share a ditch.

Brenfen (BREN-fen) is a causeway town on the peat flats east of the Ardenford
lands. In 3200 A.F. it is one of about a dozen small, rival, self-governing human
city-states in the region; there is no federation, no Haven, no Charter for another
nine centuries. Whether it survives as a town by 4327 A.F. is undecided.

## The fen wardens

The fen takes bodies slowly and gives them back wrong. Every parish keeps a
grave-keeper, a fen warden, who walks the bog at dawn and after storms, stakes or
burns what the peat returns, and keeps the book of who went in and when. The
council tolerates the trade because the alternative walks. Raising the dead is
feared here, not illegal; nobody has written a law because nobody wants to say the
word.

## Notable people

- **Caven Calderon** — a fen warden's son who learned his letters from the ledger
  of the drowned and read his way out. See his Foundry sheet (one-shot, 3200 A.F.).

## Era notes (GM)

  `history/`. Brenfen's fate in that era is unwritten.
