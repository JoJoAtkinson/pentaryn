# Undercave Smuggler

**HP** 11 (2d8+2) **·** **AC** 12 (oilskin and a leather jerkin) **·** **Speed** 30 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 10 **·** **Languages** Common, Thieves' cant **·** **CR** 1/8 (25 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

**Who they are:** dock-hands. They stack crates in straw, they know the tide table by heart, and the worst thing any of them has done is move good spirit past a customs desk. They are not getting rich and they are not dying for eleven crates.

**Per variant** (see [`_overview.md`](../_overview.md)) — mechanically identical in all three:

- **v1 — The Run:** anonymous crew.
- **v2 — The Other Cargo:** from the moment Iselle speaks, they stop trying to win and start trying to get a boat in the water. Every turn is spent moving toward the skiffs or covering someone who is.
- **v3 — Sarn Kestle:** they have names, and Sarn uses them out loud, in combat: **Onnet**, **Bray**, **little Pell**, and **Hass** — Hass being the one stuck up on the plank walkway holding a lantern.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **At or below 6 HP? Is Sarn down? Is half the crew down?** → **Throw Down Arms.** Each one breaks on its own — no order needed, no permission asked.
2. **Has a PC killed anyone?** No → **Club**, nonlethal (a PC dropped by it is unconscious and **stable**). Yes → **Scimitar**, and nobody surrenders for the rest of the fight.
3. **Crossbow loaded?** One shot from cover, then it closes or it breaks. It does not stand in the open reloading.
4. **Footing:** Dashed, Pushed, or knocked Prone on the shelf → **[ASK PLAYER] DC 11 Dex save** or Prone and sliding 5 ft seaward. Off a plank walkway is 1d6 bludgeoning into cold water.

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Round 1:** it was **working**, not waiting. It is holding a crate, a lantern, or a coil of line, and its first turn is spent dropping that and getting behind a beached skiff. **Light Crossbow** from half cover if it can manage both.
- **Round 2+:** closes to **Club** range across the wet rock. Nearest reachable PC. No flanking, no focus fire — it is a dock-hand in a brawl, not a soldier.
- **It fights to keep the party off the crates and off the boats**, in that order, and it will give up the crates long before the boats.
- **It knows the planks and the party doesn't.** If it can pull a melee PC up onto a 5-ft plank walkway with no rail, it will, and it will be pleased about it.
- **When the stirges drop:** it swats and swears and keeps fighting the party — but a stirge that attaches to *it* gets its full attention, and for a round the crew and the party have the same problem. Play that beat.
- **At or below 6 HP, or once Sarn is down, or half the crew:** **Throw Down Arms.** Weapon in the water, hands up, backs toward a skiff.
- **A broken crew makes for the boats, not the exit.** They push off into the inlet. There is nothing at the end of chasing them.
- **If a surrendered smuggler is attacked:** surrender is over for everyone. Survivors fight to 0 HP and the boats stop mattering.

## Description (one line)

A wiry dock-hand in wet oilskin with straw still stuck to his forearms, holding a boat-hook haft in both hands and glancing past the party at the tide line, doing arithmetic about how long this can go on.
