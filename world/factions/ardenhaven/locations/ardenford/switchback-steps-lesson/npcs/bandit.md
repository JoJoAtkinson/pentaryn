# Switchback Neighbour

**HP** 11 (2d8+2) **·** **AC** 12 (leather and a work apron) **·** **Speed** 30 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 10 **·** **Languages** Common, Thieves' cant **·** **CR** 1/8 (25 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

**Who they are:** depends on the variant — see [`_overview.md`](../_overview.md). Mechanically identical either way.

- **v1 — The Lesson:** Calderwick's four lodgers. They rent his upper floor. They are not here out of love; they are here because the stair is the only way in or out of the building they live in.
- **v2 / v3 — The Neighbours:** *not* lodgers and *not* muscle. A joiner, two dock-hands, and a woman who bakes, who came out of their own doors carrying whatever was beside the door — because a Calderon refugee being jumped on a stair by strangers is the exact shape of the thing every one of them left the Imperium to escape. None of them wanted this and all four of them are here. **In v2 and v3 they say nothing at all, the entire fight.** Only Veska speaks.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **Is the rule still on?** Nobody has been killed → this one uses **Club**, and its damage is **nonlethal** (a PC dropped by it is unconscious and **stable**, no death saves). The instant a PC kills anyone on this stair, drop the club, draw the **Scimitar**, and it is lethal from there on — for everybody.
2. **Is it below 6 HP, or is Veska down, or are two of its side down?** → **Throw Down Arms**. Each one breaks on its own; they do not wait for permission.
3. **Footing:** if it Dashed, was Pushed, or was knocked Prone on a *flight* (not a landing) — 1d4 × 5 ft downhill and 1d6 bludgeoning. This applies to them exactly as much as to the party.

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Round 1:** it does not charge. Two come down the flight above and two up the flight below, and they **stand there**, filling the width of the stair. The party is surrounded before a blow is struck, on 5 ft of wet stone, and that is the position they wanted.
- **Every round after:** **Club** the nearest PC. No flanking (there is no room), no focus fire, no target priority. It hits whoever is in front of it because whoever is in front of it is all it can reach.
- **It fights to hold ground, not to advance.** It will not step off its tread to chase a PC further up or down the stair. Push past it and it lets you go and closes the gap behind you.
- **It will not touch a downed PC.** Once a PC is at 0 and stable it steps over them, and it will step over a PC who is *pretending* too. These are not people who kick someone on the ground; that is the country they left.
- **Below 6 HP:** **Throw Down Arms.** Weapon on the stone, hands open, backs up two treads.
- **If a surrendered one is attacked:** the rule is over. Every survivor on the stair draws steel and fights to 0 HP. Nobody yields after that.
- **If the party withdraws to Western Market Lane:** it does not follow. It stands on the stair until they are out of sight, and then it goes and checks on Calderwick.

## Description (one line)

A tired man in a flour-dusted apron holding a joiner's mallet in both hands like a tool he has never once thought of as a weapon, standing in the middle of a wet stair because the alternative is letting strangers up it.
