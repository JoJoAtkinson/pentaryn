# Goblin Raider

**HP** 10 (3d6) **·** **AC** 15 (leather armor, shield) **·** **Speed** 30 ft. **·** **Save** Dex +2 **·** **Stealth** +6 **·** **Darkvision** 60 ft. **·** **Passive Perception** 9 **·** **CR** 1/4 (50 XP)

> Action mechanics live in `combat-runner/actions.jsonl` — see the launcher's **Ready actions** reference for verbs.

---

## Start-of-turn checklist

1. **Nimble Escape** is available every turn — a bonus-action Disengage or Hide. Use it.
2. If this raider is below 5 HP, or the boss has fallen, it wants out (Disengage + Dash for the cave or open ground).

---

## Tactics — when the DM asks "what does it do?"

- **Round 1:** loose a shortbow shot from behind boulder cover, then Nimble Escape to stay hidden.
- **Kite:** never stand and trade. Shoot, Hide/Disengage, reposition — make the party come to the rocks.
- **In melee:** scimitar only if cornered; it would rather Disengage away and go back to the bow.
- **Gang up:** if two goblins flank one PC, their attacks have Advantage (+1d4 damage on a hit) — call it out.
- **Cowardice:** below 5 HP, or once the boss drops or half the band is down, it breaks and runs.

## Variant notes — see [`_overview.md`](../_overview.md)

**Count is 4 in v1 and v2, 3 in v3.** 50 XP each. Put the fourth back in any variant (**450 XP**) for a table of six.

- **v1 — The Second Warband:** as above. Survivors break for the back chamber and scatter.
- **v2 — Ghask's Word:** **one of the four is Nit** — one ear, small even for a goblin, the goblin who surrendered in session 1 and was let go. He is the reason Ghask's parley is researched rather than chanced. **During the parley the raiders do not attack** — every turn is `nimble_escape` to Hide and take a flanking angle, and they all loose at once when the betrayal fires (**first shot only at Advantage**). Nit is the only one not repositioning: he stands still, watches the party's faces, and looks ill. **If the party addresses him directly — by name, or about last time — he breaks**, steps out of his firing lane, and the ambush loses a shooter and all of its surprise. He will not fight his own band; he goes over the rubble and away.
- **v3 — The Fifth Goblin:** only three of them, and the fourth is not out foraging. **Nothing retreats into the back passage in this variant, for any reason** — broken raiders run *outward*, over the rubble into the open scrubland at dusk, which is where the fight ends. If a stirge latches onto one of them, it screams for the boss and it is not acting.

## Description (one line)

A wiry, leather-clad goblin with a notched scimitar and a shortbow, quick to dart between the rubble.
