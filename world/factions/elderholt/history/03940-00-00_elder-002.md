Elder Calyrix strikes an unprecedented bargain to be present wherever witch-children learn. She fractures into dozens of lesser selves, each anchored to a place in Elderstead—statues, mirrors, doorframes, and teaching circles. She becomes distributed consciousness rather than a single witch.
