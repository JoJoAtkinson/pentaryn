Marwen Ashcroft, now 20 years old, leaves Elderholt as a threadling to wander the world. Her first destination is the ruins near Ardenhaven, where her sister Clair once traveled. She carries Marrith's crow, Silniel's hidden seeds, and the weight of Naelith's dark prophecy.
