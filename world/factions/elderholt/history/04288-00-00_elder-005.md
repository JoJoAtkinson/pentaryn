Elder Marrith the Ashen Measure is forced to execute her own daughter Elwen, a deep-witch who crossed too many lines and could no longer be contained. Marrith draws a circle of pale fire that burns only inward, leaving nothing but ash. This judgment occurs when Breann (Marrith's granddaughter) was still a young Votary.
