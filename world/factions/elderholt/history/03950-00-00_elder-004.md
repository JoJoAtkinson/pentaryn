Elderholt's witches act as one in a rare unified action. Led by Kharbora and other elders, they descend into the Underdark and eliminate multiple drow matron mothers. Taramet forges a massive door to seal away a corridor to the deep unknown. The key is later lost, though Taramet seems unconcerned.
