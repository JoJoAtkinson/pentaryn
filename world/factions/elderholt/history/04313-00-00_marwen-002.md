The Elderholt elders argue for days over whether to accept Marwen as a threadling. Boys are never taken by the coven, but the fire had already unraveled what she had been. Ishvar of Prudence actively opposes her initiation. The Axiom recognizes Marwen as its own, and she is accepted.
