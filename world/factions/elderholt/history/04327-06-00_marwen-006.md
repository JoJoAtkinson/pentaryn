Shortly before leaving Elderholt, Marwen uses magic to swiftly climb Silniel's branches, no longer waiting for permission from others to act. She reaches the canopy as leaves rustle and charms clatter. Silniel has secretly placed seeds on Marwen to be carried into the wider world.
