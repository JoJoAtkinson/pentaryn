Elder Brannith the Hag begins teaching Marwen without asking permission. She has Marwen sit in a rotting doorway for hours—one foot inside Elderholt, one foot out—forcing the young witch to confront her visibility and the expectations others place on her. The lesson: "If you wait to be welcomed, you will rot in the doorway."
