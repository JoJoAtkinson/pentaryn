Clair Ashcroft strikes her first real bargain with the Voice and becomes a Tollbearer. She leaves Elderholt to wander the world, following the traditional path of witches who have outgrown the coven's teachings. Marwen plans to follow her sister's path toward the ruins near Ardenhaven.
