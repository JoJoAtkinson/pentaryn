Before Marwen departs, Elder Marrith the Ashen Measure gives her a crow to travel with her. The crow is tasked with watching for corrupted witches—those who have lost themselves to the Voice. Marrith warns Marwen: if she must face such a witch, send word immediately and do not act alone.
