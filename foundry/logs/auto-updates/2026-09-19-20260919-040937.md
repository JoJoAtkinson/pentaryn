## Worth knowing

**foundry-mcp-bridge 0.8.4** ([release](https://github.com/adambdooley/foundry-vtt-mcp/releases/tag/v0.8.4))
- **New: scene music.** You can bind playlists and tracks to scenes, create, edit and delete playlists, and control playback.
- **New: ActiveEffect management.** You can create, update and delete effects on actors or on their items.
- **New: `manage-actors` has a `place` action.** It drops an existing world actor onto the current scene, so you don't have to create a new one.
- **New: `replace-journal-page` tool.** DSA5 support was also added, but it doesn't matter for this table.
- **Fixes:**
  - `use-item` targeting on V13/V14
  - Large responses timing out over WebRTC
  - Several D&D 5e feature-creation defects
  - Loopback hosts now use `ws://` even on HTTPS pages
  - Renaming journal pages
  - Scene background images after `generate-map`

**multi-token-edit 3.3.4 → 3.3.6**

- The Levels migration now includes BW Stair migration (3.3.4) and has other improvements (3.3.5).
- 3.3.6 fixed presets always spawning at elevation 0 on 3D Canvas scenes.
