## Held

### [dnd5e](https://github.com/foundryvtt/dnd5e/releases) 5.3.3 → 6.0.5 (major)

The system's own notes call 6.0 its largest release ever. It was held because it reworks how chat-card data is stored and how ActiveEffects work, and because **Argon combat HUD (`enhancedcombathud-dnd5e`) and Item Piles (`itempilesdnd5e`) may break**. You're already on Foundry v14, which 6.0 requires. The 6.0.0 notes in the record are cut off, so read the full release page before deciding.

New in 6.0 that's worth trying:
- **Falling and fall damage are automatic** on maps that use Levels.
- **ActiveEffects v2** adds conditional effects, rules-based effects and effects attached to Regions (e.g. a hazard zone that applies its effect itself). Effect expiry handling is also improved.
- **Smaller chat cards.** They now show the description of the specific action used rather than the whole item's. Targets are grouped; right-click a target to adjust it or pan to it.
- **Effects tray:** shift+click applies several effects at once. The damage tray also groups targets, and you can set per-target multipliers from the right-click menu.
- **Better armor class handling** (one of the long-requested features).
- 6.0.2 lets you add custom flavour text to group roll requests. 6.0.4 shows resolved dice in roll dialogs when a formula has fixed computed values.

The 6.0.1–6.0.5 patches fixed some serious early bugs:
- some worlds failed to load (6.0.1)
- casting a concentration spell from a scroll caused an infinite loop (6.0.2)
- effects with no duration expired after one turn in combat (6.0.3)
- creatures immune to exhaustion still got exhaustion level II (6.0.4)
- rolling a check or save requested by an action could throw an error (6.0.5)

The fast patch cadence (five patches in 12 days) suggests 6.x is still settling.

**What you'd need to do:**
1. Confirm Argon combat HUD and Item Piles have v6-compatible releases.
2. Update during a quiet week, not right before a session. The migration can't be undone except by restoring a snapshot.
3. If you ever installed the 6.0 pre-release, the release notes say you **must uninstall and reinstall** the system first, or data may be lost.
